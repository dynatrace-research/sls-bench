assumptions:
  - |
    The multi-day outage is compressed into a 50-minute scenario while preserving the causal arc (wraparound shutdown → retries/spooling/disk pressure → vacuum → truncation → vacuum completion and partial recovery/backlog drain).
  - |
    The reported "only ~80% of queued emails sent" is modeled as an 80/20 shard split: non-shard4 sends continue; shard4-mapped sends fail until recovery.
  - |
    Postgres does not log every query; per-request outcomes are modeled in application logs, while Postgres emits background rejection and vacuum telemetry plus discrete maintenance markers.
  - |
    XID telemetry is split into an incident-high snapshot (xid_metric) and a post-maintenance snapshot (xid_metric_post) so the scenario can suppress the former after recovery without contradicting the narrative.
  - |
    Normal-phase expected volume is dominated by send flows (~1260 flow-logs/min) with light background telemetry (~12 logs/min).
  - |
    Total expected log volume over the full scenario is ~70k–90k depending on retry amplification multipliers, within the 20k..100k target range.
  - |
    Key diagnostic visibility comes from shard4 wraparound shutdown (one-shot), shard4 connection rejections (high-rate background), API shard4 write failures/retries plus spooling flows, disk/queue pressure metrics, and maintenance markers (vacuum start/progress, truncations, vacuum completion, and alert-rule addition).
