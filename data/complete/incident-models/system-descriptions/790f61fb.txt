assumptions:
  - "GitHub is modeled as traffic entering at the load balancer; the webhook delivery pause then surge is represented by github_webhook_enqueue_ok having 0 rpm in normal and high rpm in failure."
  - "API request paths include an explicit api_service return hop before edge_lb to keep path connectivity without modeling a DB->LB edge."
  - "Failure-onset cleanliness: failure-only background signals and failure-only error flows are suppressed in early failure minutes via scenario controls so 'initially coping' is not contradicted before DB contention starts."
  - "Normal-phase scheduler activity is kept non-zero (20 rpm) to represent manual/scheduled builds and background queue processing even while webhook delivery is paused."
  - "The nonlinear DB degradation is represented by activating postgres_db.lock_alert/postgres_db.query_timeout/postgres_db.slow_query plus increased enqueue/dequeue latencies and a scheduler_service.queue_pressure snapshot stream."
  - "LB operational one-shots are split into edge_lb.capacity_throttle vs edge_lb.capacity_rollback to prevent generated logs from contradicting event-time actions."
  - "Auto re-enqueue disable is modeled as a dedicated one-shot ops_repl.auto_reenqueue_disabled (fixed semantics) to prevent contradictory values during the mitigation event."
  - "The post-mortem step of pulling slow query logs is represented by ops_repl.collect_slow_queries as a one-shot action marker, separate from postgres_db.slow_query background emission."
  - "The complex usage/run queue state is represented via scheduler_service.queue_stats (run_q/usage_q) plus ops_repl drain script logs rather than distinct storage subsystems."
  - "Retry semantics for webhook delivery are modeled as separate inbound requests (RPM), not as in-request retries; therefore webhook flows have max_attempts=1."
  - "LB access logging is modeled as completion-style and emitted after API completion; response-return hops are represented by repeating api_service then edge_lb at the end of API-request paths."
  - "Normal-phase volume estimate (with the scenario rates): ~330–370 logs/min (dominated by customer_api_ok, scheduler_dequeue_ok, and builder heartbeats)."
  - "Whole-scenario total log estimate (for the scenario timing/rates): ~33k–50k logs, staying within the 20k..100k target by using 1–3 lines per request and moderate failure RPMs."
  - "Key diagnostic anchors are emission-backed and likely visible: scheduler dequeue_timeouts and collapsing dequeue_ok rate, postgres_db lock_alert/query_timeout/slow_query turning on, LB access 503s during throttling, and ops one-shots for stepdown/patch/drain actions."
