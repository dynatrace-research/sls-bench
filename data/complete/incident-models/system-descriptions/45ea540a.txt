assumptions:
  - >-
    The original multi-hour incident is compressed to a single 50-minute scenario while preserving causal order
    (replica network loss to full-sync load to bad Redis restart to balances lost/read-only to auto-recharge spike
    to billing taken offline to brief re-enable to recurrence and offline again).
  - >-
    Voice and SMS delivery are abstracted as a single high-rate usage_ingest→billing_api flow; communications delivery
    is out of scope except for generating billing events.
  - >-
    Auto-recharge triggering is split into two explicit log/flow variants to keep the recurrence diagnostically consistent:
    immediately after the wipe, recharge is requested with reason=zero_balance; after a ledger-based restore/re-enable,
    recharge can reappear with reason=balance_unwritable (unsafe behavior when Redis is READONLY even if the ledger is correct).
  - >-
    Gateway and worker result logs are split into approved/declined variants to keep suspension signals consistent with
    card declines and reduce semantic ambiguity in generated logs.
  - >-
    Traffic sizing uses ~250 rpm usage so that combined flow + background emissions produce ~55k logs over 50 minutes
    (within the 20k-100k target).
  - >-
    Visibility anchors include replica link_down/fullsync (trigger), redis_master replica_sync_backlog/command_timeout
    (overload), redis_master restart+aof_open_failed+role_changed/role_status (bad restart + persistent readonly),
    billing_api balance_write_readonly with low-rate recharge_requested_* (customer-impacting path),
    billing_monitor anomaly_detected/alert_sent (detection), and billing_mode_changed with pricing_deferred_offline
    (mitigation).
