assumptions:
  - >
    The real outage lasted ~10 hours; this model compresses it into a 60-minute scenario while preserving the causal core:
    wraparound autovacuum + a long-lived pending exclusive-lock DDL (DROP TRIGGER) creates head-of-line blocking for shared-lock
    metadata queries, producing partial API timeouts/500s.
  - >
    The long-lived nature of the DROP TRIGGER waiter is modeled explicitly as a failure-state background signal
    (pg_shard_a.ddl_lock_wait_drop_trigger with wait_s up to 30000), rather than as many short-lived client attempts.
    This keeps pg_monitor.pg_stats_bad's exclusive_pending=1 realistic over extended degraded intervals.
  - >
    API-side metadata timeouts are modeled with a fixed 20s upstream timeout (timeout_ms==waited_ms==20000), and the overall 500
    response duration is constrained to ~20s plus small overhead (manta_api.http_5xx.dur_ms up to ~20500; flow step hints align),
    avoiding contradictory per-request chronology.
  - >
    api_metrics is emitted per API host once per minute; rps is modeled per-host (~0.8-2.5 rps) to align with ~300 rpm total traffic
    across three API hosts. Failure-state api_metrics ranges are widened to allow either degraded or recovered values during mitigations.
  - >
    Only two shards are modeled: shard A (affected) and shard B (healthy), sufficient to reproduce ~20% overall errors
    (60 rpm timeouts out of 300 rpm total at failure baseline).
  - >
    Normal-phase log volume estimate: ~300 rpm * 2 logs/req + background (~5 logs/min) ≈ ~605 logs/min.
  - >
    Total scenario log volume estimate: ~36k-40k logs (flows dominate; additional monitor/lock-wait background and a few
    operator one-shots), within the 20k..100k target.
  - >
    Diagnostic visibility anchors are emission-backed and frequent: manta_api.pg_query_timeout/http_5xx (impact),
    pg_monitor.pg_stats_bad/pg_alert (lock pileup + exclusive pending), pg_shard_a.ddl_lock_wait_drop_trigger and autovacuum markers
    (mechanism), and ops_tool restart/prefetch/patch/config-tune one-shots (operator actions and phase boundaries).
