  id: sessionstore_cassandra_quorum_outage
  desc: >
    MediaWiki application hosts rely on a SessionStore HTTP service to read/write user session data.
    SessionStore persists sessions in a Cassandra cluster spanning two datacenters and uses QUORUM consistency for session operations.
    When enough Cassandra nodes go down (here due to disk exhaustion), SessionStore cannot reach quorum and begins returning HTTP 500s to MediaWiki.
    A disruptive mitigation is to wipe/truncate the session keyspace to free disk, restart Cassandra, and restore availability at the cost of losing all sessions.
