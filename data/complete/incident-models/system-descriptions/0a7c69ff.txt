assumptions:
  - >
    The real incident lasted multiple hours; the scenario compresses it into 50 minutes while preserving the onset,
    partial mitigations, worsening due to additional device failures, and a mitigation test disabling a protocol in one AZ.
  - >
    Direct Connect is packet-based; to keep logs tractable, traffic is modeled as aggregated "batches" per connection,
    where loss/latency metrics summarize many packets.
  - >
    To make the protocol-disable mitigation causally consistent in logs, AZ2 traffic is explicitly modeled with two
    mutually exclusive flow families: fast protocol (fwd_batch_result_fast_az2 / device_fault_fast_az2) before the disable,
    and legacy convergence (fwd_batch_result_legacy_az2) after the disable. Scenario events suppress the fast-AZ2 flows
    once protocol_disabled is applied, preventing post-disable logs from claiming fast-converge-v2 in AZ2.
  - >
    The remaining degradation at the end of the scenario is attributed to AZ1 continuing to use the fast protocol
    (fwd_batch_result / device_fault), while AZ2 shows improvement after the single-AZ mitigation.
  - >
    noc_alerting.alarm_packet_loss is a true alarm log and is suppressed in normal (per_min=0.0) to avoid contradicting
    the alarm wording with healthy loss values.
  - >
    To keep dx_health_automation.auto_isolation_skipped semantically consistent, threshold is fixed at 3 and failed_devices
    is constrained to be >= 3, matching the message claim that auto-isolation was deferred due to exceeding a safety threshold.
  - >
    Normal-phase expected volume: ~21k logs over 25 minutes (dominant: dx_customer_batch_normal and dx_probe_batch_normal).
  - >
    Total expected scenario volume: ~41k logs (normal ~21k + failure ~20k), within the 20k..100k target.
  - >
    Key diagnostic visibility comes from frequent noc_alerting.alarm_packet_loss (failure only), elevated
    dx_transport_layer.drop_metric, intermittent device_fault logs (AZ1 and pre-disable AZ2), and
    dx_health_automation.auto_isolation_skipped, plus the explicit protocol switch visible as
    fwd_batch_result_fast_az2 disappearing and fwd_batch_result_legacy_az2 appearing after mitigation.
