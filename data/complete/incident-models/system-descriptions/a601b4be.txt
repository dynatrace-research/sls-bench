sys:
  id: cloudflare_recursive_dns_1111_oct2023
  desc: >
    A recursive DNS service (modeled after 1.1.1.1) answers client DNS queries and validates DNSSEC.
    To speed resolution and provide resilience, edge resolvers use a locally served copy of the DNS root zone (RFC 7706)
    provided by an embedded WebAssembly app ("static_zone").
    Root zone updates are pulled and distributed internally, but a parser limitation prevents edge nodes from adopting
    new root zone versions containing a newly introduced record type (ZONEMD), leaving some nodes on a stale root zone
    until its DNSSEC signatures expire and SERVFAIL responses increase.

states: {n: normal, f: failure}
