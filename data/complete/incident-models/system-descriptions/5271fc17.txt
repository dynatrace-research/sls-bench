    Failure-onset cleanliness: mitigation/recovery-only flows (kv_get_bypass_ok, access_auth_ok_after_bypass, http_user_5xx_recovery_tail) are set to 0 rpm in failure baseline so they cannot appear at incident onset; mitigations are evidenced via one-shot markers and reduced failure rates instead of sustained bypass-success request logs.
  - |
    A recovery tail is primarily represented by sharply reducing bot-panic request volume plus proxy restart markers, rather than by a separate high-volume recovery-tail flow.
  - |
    Normal traffic volume is scaled down for log generation: ~240 rpm user success plus small baseline errors; this still yields realistic phase signals and keeps total logs within target.
  - |
    Rough volume estimate over a 56-minute scenario: normal ~1125 logs/min * 28 min ≈ 31k; failure ~760–1515 logs/min * 28 min ≈ 38k; total ≈ 69k–75k logs including one-shots (within 20k..100k).
