assumptions:
  - |
    Names are genericized; "coordinator" represents the ZooKeeper-like quorum service, and "event_queue" abstracts the durable store used to buffer notifications.
  - |
    Three datacenters are modeled as aws_r1, aws_r2, and linode; quorum requires at least 2 of 3 coordinator nodes to be mutually reachable.
  - |
    Only a small subset of API endpoints is modeled: /v1/events (write path) stays healthy; a read endpoint shows intermittent 500s to reflect broader platform impact without adding extra components.
  - |
    coordinator.quorum_lost is treated as a discrete cluster-state transition log (emitted via scenario one_shots) rather than a continuous background stream, to support a clear phase boundary when quorum is first lost.
  - |
    Normal-phase expected log volume is dominated by post_event (~300 rpm, 2 logs) and dispatch_notification (~200 rpm, 3 logs): roughly ~1.2k logs/min plus background.
  - |
    Total scenario log volume is designed to be ~70k (within 20k..100k) by keeping request RPM moderate and limiting per-request logs.
  - |
    Diagnostic visibility anchors: net_probe.ping_metric (early network degradation), coordinator.session_expired (quorum mechanism pressure), dispatch_worker.job_requeued + coordinator.quorum_fail (dispatch stop), coordinator.quorum_lost (quorum transition marker), event_queue.queue_depth_metric/snapshot (backlog growth), and monitoring.alert_multi_provider_failure (provider-facing symptom corroboration).
