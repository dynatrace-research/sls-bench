  - "The post-mortem is modeled as a parent-order router (broker gateway) feeding a SMARS engine cluster and an exchange gateway, with executions summarized by periodic metrics rather than per-child-order logs."
  - "The one-of-eight servers missing the new code is represented by a distinct Power Peg acceptance/warn pair fixed to engine=8 and by config_auditor.deploy_audit_partial (7 updated, 1 stale)."
  - "To match the rollback/uninstall spreading legacy behavior, a second Power Peg flow/log pair (accept_order_powerpeg_legacy/powerpeg_warn_legacy) allows engine IDs 1–8; scenario events suppress it pre-rollback and enable it post-rollback."
  - "smars_router.minute_stats parent_orders and child_orders_sent domains are aligned to the modeled parent-order RPM (~150/min cluster-wide) and slice counts (1–60), while still allowing extreme child-order counts during Power Peg runaway via a widened failure-state domain."
  - "Normal-phase log volume estimate is ~900 flow logs/min (150 rpm × 6) plus ~16 background logs/min, yielding ~18k–19k logs over 20 minutes."
  - "Total scenario log volume estimate is ~42k–47k logs (within 20k–100k), driven primarily by per-parent-order gateway/SMARS/exchange acknowledgements plus per-minute summaries."
  - "Visibility rationale: pre-rollback Power Peg activation is visible via smars_router.powerpeg_warn (engine=8) plus exec_summary_minute; post-rollback spread is visible via smars_router.powerpeg_warn_legacy (engine varies) plus config_auditor.deploy_audit_rollback; business impact is corroborated by exchange exec_summary_spike and risk_monitor.limit_breach without inflating rare client-visible symptoms."
