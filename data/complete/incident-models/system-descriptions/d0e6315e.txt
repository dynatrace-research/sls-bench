  id: edge_dns_rrdns
  desc: >
    An edge authoritative DNS service answers queries for customer domains and performs on-demand CNAME
    resolution to discover origin IPs. For CNAME targets, the edge uses an internal recursive resolver pool

    on a subset of hosts, a non-monotonic time delta could become negative and, after smoothing, feed a
    negative weight into upstream selection, triggering a panic that was recovered but caused SERVFAIL.

assumptions:
  - >
    The real system spans many data centers; this model compresses it to four edge hosts and two resolver hosts
    while preserving the causal mechanism (clock step backward → negative RTT samples → smoothed resolver stats drift negative → panic in weighted selection → SERVFAIL for some CNAME lookups).
  - >
    Upstream selection (upstream_selected) is assumed to be logged only on successful selection; therefore its failure-state srtt_ms domain is non-negative even during the incident.
    Negative smoothed RTT visibility is modeled via a separate periodic anomaly log (rrdns_metrics_neg) that can be enabled later in the failure via scenario controls.
  - >
    Only DNS is modeled directly; downstream HTTP error impact is noted in the scenario but not modeled as separate HTTP flows.
  - >
    Normal-phase volume estimate: ~1330 logs/min from flows (280 rpm *2 + 108 rpm *6 + 12 rpm *10) + ~2 background logs/min ≈ 1332 logs/min.
  - >
    End-to-end scenario volume estimate (with the scenario timings): ~70k–80k total logs, within the 20k–100k target, dominated by per-request DNS logs; extra anomaly-metric lines add only tens of logs.
  - >
    Monitoring alert domains are constrained so alert value is always above the possible threshold range (no semantically contradictory alert-open logs).
