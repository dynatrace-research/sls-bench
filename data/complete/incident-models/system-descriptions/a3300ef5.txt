assumptions:
  - |
    Time is compressed to a 50-minute scenario; real incident and restoration lasted many hours.
  - |
    Git repositories/wikis are stored outside PostgreSQL and are not modeled; the incident focuses on metadata DB loss and service outage.
  - |
    pg_dump backups are modeled as running about once every 5 minutes (rpm=0.2) to ensure the version-mismatch signal appears in a single log file;
    in reality they were daily and failing over a longer period.
  - |
    DMARC mail rejection is modeled as a low-rate background signal on the mail relay (rather than a hop in the pg_dump flow path) to keep path
    connectivity valid while still showing that failure notifications are not delivered.
  - |
    Failure-state background logs for WAL removal and startup failures are modeled as periodic emissions to represent repeated occurrences
    (WAL cleanup under lag; repeated restart attempts after data deletion).
  - |
    Rough log volume estimate over 50 minutes: normal ~36k; failure ~40k-50k; total ~75k-90k (within the 20k-100k target).
