  - |-
      Time is compressed: the multi-hour regional event is modeled as a ~34-minute incident preserving the causal
      sequence (connection storm → edge congestion → DNS/API/monitoring impairment → partial mitigations).
  - |-
      The "internal network" vs "main network" boundary is represented only by the edge_gateway component; downstream
      services are simplified to internal_dns and ec2_control_plane plus a scaled capacity_service endpoint.
  - |-
      Reverse-direction connectivity (e.g., internal_dns -> edge_gateway) is modeled explicitly as component "to" edges
      to make request paths with return hops traversable for log generation.
  - |-
      Retry semantics: flow emit chains are per-entry-request, so internal_client_connect_ok is modeled as single-attempt successes,
      while internal_client_connect_fail includes retries to represent connection churn amplification. The backoff bug is
      represented by smaller failure-state backoff_ms in internal_client_fleet.connect_retry and higher expected_attempts in
      internal_client_connect_fail during failure.
  - |-
      edge_gateway.nat_stats and edge_gateway.conntrack_overflow counters represent aggregate gateway load including
      unmodeled traffic; their quantitative envelopes were reduced to remain broadly consistent with the modeled traffic
      scale while still showing clear incident-time elevation.
  - |-
      DNS health snapshot qps domains are aligned to the modeled DNS flow volumes (flows represent a small modeled subset of
      cross-network DNS traffic for this incident-specific spec).
  - |-
      Variable envelopes were adjusted to avoid contradictions with modeled latency hints (e.g., dns_ok/reply_ok dur_ms and
      http_200_run/rpc_ok_run dur_ms cover slower success cases during congestion).
  - |-
      Normal-phase log volume estimate: ~17k logs over 16 minutes (dominated by EC2 API calls and routine internal DNS resolutions).
  - |-
      Total scenario log volume estimate: ~55k–70k logs (target 20k–100k), dominated by failure-phase DNS/EC2 flows and
      internal-client connection failures with retries; edge/monitoring background signals provide corroborating time-series evidence.
  - |-
      Visibility rationale: edge_gateway.nat_stats/conntrack_overflow show congestion, internal_dns.reply_servfail shows DNS impact,
      ec2_api_frontend.http_503_* shows customer-facing control-plane errors, and monitoring_pipeline.metric_ingest_lag shows reduced
