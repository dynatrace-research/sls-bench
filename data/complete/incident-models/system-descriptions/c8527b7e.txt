  - "Model focuses on the public-IP programming pipeline (RM -> NCP -> canary -> L2 edge) as the incident causal core."

  - "Control-plane apply success is logged only after edge programming logs, to preserve known-at-this-time chronology."
  - "Real incident lasted ~2h; scenario time-compresses while preserving ordering: trigger -> canary timeout/queueing -> worker restarts zone-by-zone -> partial drain."
  - "Normal-phase expected volume is ~1120 logs/min dominated by create_resource_ok and health_check_ok; low background metrics provide baseline markers."
  - "Estimated total scenario log volume is ~50k-60k, within the 20k-100k target; key diagnostics (canary_timeout, ncp_queue_status_*, l2lb_drop_nomap) are frequent enough to be visible."
