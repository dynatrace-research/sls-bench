assumptions:
  - |
    Repair note (S8): failover_metrics_1m failure-state domains were widened to admit early/low values as well as later storm values,
    and the scenario suppresses these metrics at failure onset and enables them during the storm window to match the stated progression.
  - |
    Repair note (S7): Failover rate-limit values and failover task rates were aligned so that the rate-limit reduction is meaningful.
    Failover task emission in failure is modeled at tens-to-hundreds per minute during the storm window, and the rate-limit change log uses
    old_per_min/new_per_min domains consistent with those rates.
  - |
    The modeled system focuses on the causal core: stale internal DNS -> collection-agent connection failures -> memory leak on storage hosts -> I/O timeouts and failover storm, plus a later overly aggressive API throttling policy affecting Describe* calls.
  - |
    Downstream customer services impacted by stuck volumes (e.g., RDS/ELB) are omitted to keep the model minimal; their effects are represented indirectly by elevated I/O timeouts and failover activity in the AZ.
  - |
    The “fraction of hosts with stale DNS” is simplified as most modeled storage hosts showing agent connection errors in failure; this is sufficient to produce correlated memory pressure and stuck volumes.
  - |
    For failover_task_enqueued, dst_host is modeled as a separate pool of candidate hosts (ebs-a4..ebs-a6) to ensure src_host != dst_host and keep the log semantics coherent without adding extra components/flows.
  - |
    Throttling policy changes are modeled with policy-specific action markers and policy-specific 429 logs (aggressive vs relaxed) to avoid contradictory carrier values in the incident timeline.
  - |
    Failover rate-limit changes are modeled with a reduction-specific marker whose domains ensure old_per_min > new_per_min in all generated logs.
  - |
    Normal-phase expected volume (25 min): ~840–870 logs/min (mostly flow logs) => ~21k–22k logs.
  - |
    Whole-scenario expected volume (60 min): ~45k–52k logs depending on failure multipliers; within the 20k–100k target.
  - |
    Visibility anchors: ebs_storage.agent_report_err (stale collector-a), ebs_storage.host_mem_sample/low_free_mem (agent RSS growth),
    ebs_storage.io_timeout (stuck I/O), ebs_failover_mgr.failover_task_enqueued & failover_metrics_1m (failover storm),
    api_frontend.api_resp_429_aggressive/api_resp_429_relaxed plus throttle_policy_set_* (throttling window and change).
