assumptions:
  - |
    Timeline is compressed into a 50-minute scenario while preserving key beats: power-loss trigger, lock-service overload,
    handshake timeouts causing nodes to self-remove, manual timeout increase plus metadata restarts enabling storage rejoin,
    then throttling of CreateDomain/DeleteDomain.
  - |
    The model uses one API-frontend service, a metadata cluster, a storage cluster, an internal lock service, an ops control plane,
    and a facility monitor; other AWS infrastructure is omitted.
  - |
    Handshake timeout logs are split into pre- and post-mitigation variants so their deadline_ms values cannot contradict the
    ops config_change marker; scenario events suppress/activate the appropriate variant over time.
  - |
    Timeout log domains are constrained so elapsed_ms is always >= deadline_ms (to prevent contradictory "timeout" logs where
    elapsed is less than the deadline).
  - |
    API-layer retries are not modeled (max_attempts=1) to keep response-return logs (respond 500/200/503) unambiguously once-per-request;
    the post-mortem's "retries" are represented in the internal lock-handshake attempt fields and repeated timeout logs.
  - |
    Request rates are simplified to ~336 RPM total in normal (reads 240, writes 90, domain ops 6).
    Failure uses the same entry rates but shifts traffic between success/error/throttled variants via scenario multipliers.
  - |
    Throttling numeric domains are modeled to be consistent with low domain-ops volume: throttle limits are sub-0.1 rps and
    rejected_last_min ranges match the expected single-digit rejected requests per minute.
  - |
    Normal-phase log volume is approximately 1.34k flow logs per minute plus ~24 background logs per minute (~1.36k per minute).
  - |
    Total scenario log volume estimate is approximately ~58k–62k logs, within the 20k–100k target.
  - |
    Key diagnostic visibility comes from lock_service.queue_warn and lock_service.deregister_batch (overload),
    metadata_node/storage_node handshake_timeout_pre (propagation pre-mitigation),
    leave_cluster one-shots (cascade),
    control_plane_ops.config_change plus handshake_timeout_post suppression (mitigation),
    and API 500/503 responses (customer impact and remaining impairment).
