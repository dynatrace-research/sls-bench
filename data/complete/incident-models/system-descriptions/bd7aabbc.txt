assumptions:
  - "The model represents one affected Lambda cell (cell_id use1-cell-07) in us-east-1; other cells are omitted."
  - "Async invocation is modeled as a request that returns status=202 on acceptance (lambda_frontend.async_accepted) and is confirmed by the event router (event_router.delivery_success). Synchronous completion remains status=200 (lambda_frontend.invoke_success)."
  - "The latent defect is modeled observationally as a persistent divergence between allocated and ready/in-use capacity (invocation_manager.pool_stats) plus per-request provisioning blocks."
  - "Dependent services are simplified to an async event router (EventBridge-like) and an STS-like SAML federation path to show downstream impact without modeling every AWS service mentioned."
  - "Normal-phase volume estimate is ~1.05k request/attempt logs per minute plus ~5 background logs per minute across hosts."
  - "Total scenario volume estimate over 50 minutes is ~65k-75k logs driven primarily by sync invoke traffic and retries, within the 20k-100k target."
  - "Visibility rationale: key diagnostics (lambda_frontend.frontend_metrics, invocation_manager.pool_stats/provision_blocked, event_router.queue_stats, and lambda_frontend.invoke_error) occur at high enough rates to appear repeatedly; mitigation actions are one-shot markers."
