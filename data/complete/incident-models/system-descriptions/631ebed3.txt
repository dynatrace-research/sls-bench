assumptions:
  - |
    Timeline is compressed to fit a sub-hour scenario: the pre-open BNET email burst occurs minutes before active order flow rather than ~90 minutes earlier.
  - |
    SMARS runs on eight hosts; during early failure the generator should route most Power Peg-triggering orders to smars8 with build=legacy_v1, while other hosts run rlp_v2.
  - |
    After the modeled "rollback", the generator should emit build=legacy_v1 on all SMARS hosts, making nearly all parent orders follow the Power Peg flow variant.
  - |
    Execution volume is modeled via aggregated per-parent-order summaries and per-minute SMARS stats, not per-trade logs, to keep volume realistic for analysis.
  - |
    order_gateway.respond_ok.dur_ms is widened to cover worst-case modeled end-to-end latencies under Power Peg (including event latency multipliers), so duration fields remain coherent with degraded flows.
  - |
    Normal-phase rough log volume: ~180 rpm * 4 logs/req ≈ 720 logs/min plus ~15 logs/min background ≈ 735 logs/min.
  - |
    Total scenario log volume target: ~30k logs (within 20k..100k) driven mainly by per-request gateway/SMARS/NYSE logs plus a 97-email one-shot burst and amplified SMARS stats during failure.
  - |
    Key diagnostic visibility: BNET email burst (early warning), SMARS Power Peg activation + child order burst logs (triggered subset then widespread), NYSE volume anomaly alerts, and PMON account "33" limit breaches.
