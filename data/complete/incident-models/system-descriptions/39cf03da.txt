  - "TCP retransmissions are represented via increased request/connection durations and timeouts rather than explicit retry logs."
  - "Router tx_mbps domains are scaled to the modeled traffic slice implied by the flow rpms and per-request byte ranges (not a full multi-Gbps PoP)."

  - "Normal-phase expected volume is about 650 logs per minute from request flows plus about 18 to 20 logs per minute background (probes, router counters, cache stats, and summaries)."
