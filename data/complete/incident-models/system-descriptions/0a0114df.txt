assumptions:
  - "This model focuses on the Wednesday-style incident: an RTM server crash triggers reconnections, then client cache-clearing drives bulk history reloads that overload DB capacity."
  - "DB/auth/history failure signals are intentionally muted at failure onset: ws_connect_fail_db_f and api_fetch_history_timeout_f are suppressed at f.start_min and only enabled at a later failure event."
  - "Severe DB saturation is represented via db_cluster.pool_stats_overload (a WARN snapshot) that is suppressed early and appears later, while db_cluster.pool_stats can remain near-normal even in early failure minutes."
  - "Return hops in flow paths are represented as logical response edges (db_cluster->app_api and app_api->edge_gateway) to satisfy path connectivity without modeling every network layer."
  - "Database per-query logs are not modeled; DB visibility is via periodic pool snapshots, overload snapshots, plus API-layer timeouts and edge 503/504 access logs."
  - "Normal-phase volume estimate: ~680 flow logs/min + ~10 background logs/min ~= ~690 logs/min."
  - "End-to-end scenario volume estimate (with failure multipliers and one-shots) is ~44k–50k logs, within the 20k–100k target."
  - "Key diagnostic anchors likely visible: rtm_server.crash (one-shot), edge_gateway.ws_upgrade_fail_rtm, db_cluster.pool_stats_overload (later), app_api.history_timeout + edge_gateway.http_503 (later), and monitor.alert_rtm_down."
