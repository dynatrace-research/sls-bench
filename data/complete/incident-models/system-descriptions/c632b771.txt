assumptions:
  - >
    This model focuses on the April 9 (HostCo) incident window: site was briefly back online,
    then unauthorized hot-wallet withdrawals occurred and the site was taken down. The broader
    month-long storyline (earlier thefts, later attribution to Bob/RDP, exchange negotiations)
    is intentionally omitted to keep one diagnosable incident.
  - >
    The core's wallet_send_req log omits a destination address to avoid generating unrealistic high-volume
    placeholder addresses; normal wallet broadcast logs also omit destination to avoid coupling address formats to assets.
    Unauthorized BTC/ETH movements are modeled via explicit per-asset tx logs that carry fixed attacker addresses.
  - >
    To align with the "logs were wiped / missing" beat while still allowing continued theft, the model includes
    unobserved withdrawal flows (unauthorized_withdrawal*_unobserved) that emit no wallet-host logs; continued activity
    after forwarding stops is inferred via sec_ops anomaly alerts rather than local wallet logs.
  - >
    The malware name "udevd-bridge" is not assumed to be visible in live per-transaction logs at incident start;
    it is introduced via a later forensic scan one-shot (sec_ops.forensic_scan_found).
  - >
    Cleanup/tampering commands are split from generic privileged commands: attacker_ssh_session emits only
    non-forwarder, non-log-destructive sudo_exec commands, while later cleanup is modeled in attacker_log_wipe
    via sudo_exec_tamper plus audit_log_truncate; log forwarder stopping is modeled as a scenario one-shot.
  - >
    Wallet signing/broadcast logs are modeled as generic wallet events; explicit suspicion is represented via
    sec_ops.wallet_anomaly_alert* which flags unexpected transfers based on monitoring heuristics.
  - >
    log_aggregator.ingest_summary.sources is modeled as a stable list of configured sources; missing-data conditions
    are represented by source_gap_warn rather than changing the ingest_summary.sources field.
  - >
    Volume estimate: normal phase ~500 rpm * 5 logs/request ≈ 2,500 logs/min plus small background noise;
    failure phase adds maintenance responses (~1,500 logs/min) plus low-rate attacker activity, withdrawals (when visible),
    alerts, gap warnings after forwarding disruption, and a few one-shots. Total expected logs over the scenario ≈ 65k–80k,
    within the 20k–100k target.
  - >
    Key diagnostic anchors likely visible: maintenance enablement (exchange_core.maintenance_enabled),
    early BTC/ETH broadcasts to fixed thief addresses (hot_wallet_server.tx_broadcast_malware*),
    monitoring anomaly alerts (sec_ops.wallet_anomaly_alert*),
    SSH login with a specific key fingerprint (hot_wallet_server.ssh_login_success),
    and (after forwarding disruption) log-ingestion gaps (log_aggregator.source_gap_warn) plus
    forensic identification of udevd-bridge (sec_ops.forensic_scan_found).
