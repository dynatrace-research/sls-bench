assumptions:
  - "The real incident spanned ~4 hours; this model compresses the same causal progression into a 50-minute window while preserving ordering, symptoms, and remediation."
  - "Git/UI maintenance downtime is treated as out-of-scope noise; the modeled impact focuses on website routing failure at the gateways."
  - "The ZooKeeper namespace size (~1 million nodes) is represented via aggregated orchestrator startup/lock metrics rather than per-node logs."
  - "Kazoo's scaling bug is represented as elevated orchestrator ZooKeeper session churn and inability to complete startup; the specific EPIPE evidence is modeled as an operator strace observation one-shot rather than a frequent application log line."
  - "Late in failure, residual 503s are modeled as 'partial' routing-table convergence (http_503_no_route_partial) rather than implying the route table is fully empty."
  - "Gateway refresh failures in the main failure flow are constrained to upstream error responses (upstream_500/upstream_503) because the modeled chain reaches the orchestrator (orchestrator.zk_fetch_fail) before the gateway logs its failure."
  - "Normal-phase log volume estimate: ~1.4k logs/min (user traffic + route refresh + light metrics), ~28k logs over 20 minutes."
  - "Failure-phase log volume estimate: ~0.63k logs/min for most of early failure (empty-table 503s + refresh failures/retries + degraded gateway metrics + modest orchestrator churn) and ~1.25k logs/min late (mostly recovered user traffic + successful refreshes + residual partial 503s), totaling ~21k–24k logs over 30 minutes."
  - "Total estimated logs across scenario: ~49k–53k, fitting the 20k–100k target while keeping key diagnostic signals frequent (gateway 503s, refresh failures/retries, orchestrator startup/zk churn, later ZooKeeper max-buffer rejections, and then successful refresh/traffic restoration)."
