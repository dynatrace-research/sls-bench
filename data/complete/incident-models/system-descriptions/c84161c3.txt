assumptions:
  - |
    The real incident lasted many hours; this model compresses it into <60 minutes while preserving the two key beats:
    (1) leap-day GA transfer certificate failure causing HA timeout/reimage loops and HI escalation, and
    (2) a rushed blast remediation on a subset of clusters that introduces an incompatible HA networking plugin and causes
        widespread VM network loss and dependent-service 503s.
  - |
    The GA hotfix is represented by switching from vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail to vm_bootstrap_fixed_success_f after the blast
    update event; ga_transfer_cert_error stops once the hotfix flow becomes active.
  - |
    HA's real GA timeout was 25 minutes; the model keeps the "dominant wait then retry" behavior but compresses the effective
    waited_s/latency so multiple retries are observable within the scenario duration.
  - |
    Normal-phase estimate: ~800 logs/min (ACS token traffic dominates) over 25 minutes (~20k logs).
    Total estimate: ~61k logs across the scenario (normal ~20k, failure ~41k), within the 20k..100k target.
  - |
    Visibility anchors intended to be frequent/clear: guest_agent.ga_transfer_cert_error and host_agent.ha_ga_timeout (early failure),
    fabric_controller.fc_hi_threshold_hit and service_mgmt_api.sm_response_disabled (mid failure), and
    host_agent.ha_network_plugin_incompatible + acs_service.acs_resp_503 (secondary outage).
  - |
    Repair note: ha_ga_timeout.attempt is explicitly modeled as one of ["1","2","3"] so per-attempt timeout logs remain coherent with
    vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail.retry.max_attempts=3.
