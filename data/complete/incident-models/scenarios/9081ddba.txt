scenario:
  id: smars_powerpeg_deploy_mismatch
  title: SMARS deployment mismatch triggers runaway Power Peg routing during market open

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    A broker order gateway routes parent orders to a SMARS engine cluster which slices and submits child orders to exchanges.
    One SMARS engine is left on a stale release; when the market opens and RLP-flagged orders arrive, that engine activates legacy Power Peg behavior and emits runaway child orders.
    Operators initially miss diagnostic emails and then roll back/uninstall the working release, which spreads the legacy behavior across engines and spikes execution volume and exposure.
    The incident ends with order routing disabled (outage) and risk exposure still elevated, rather than a clean recovery.

  trigger_desc: |
    At market open, incoming RLP-eligible parent orders begin flowing at full rate. A subset of orders is routed to the one stale SMARS engine,
    where a repurposed flag activates legacy Power Peg routing without the intended cumulative tracking, causing runaway child order submission.

  time:
    total_minutes: 60
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 20}
      f: {start_min: 20, end_min: 60}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        Pre-open processing runs normally at moderate load. Parent orders are accepted and acknowledged, with routine per-minute execution summaries.
        Automated diagnostic emails with subject "SMARS diag: Power Peg disabled" are emitted frequently but are not treated as alerts.
        A low-rate deployment audit periodically reports one stale SMARS host (7 updated, 1 stale), but no operational action is taken.
      flows: [flow_parent_order_rlp]
      manifestation:
        - broker_gateway.parent_order_resp_ok
        - exchange_gateway.exec_summary_minute
        - email_notifier.smars_diag_email
        - config_auditor.deploy_audit_partial

    f:
      desc: |
        When full market traffic begins, some orders hit the stale SMARS engine and activate Power Peg behavior, producing runaway child order volume.
        Exchange execution summaries climb rapidly and venue backpressure starts to appear. Operators attempt countermeasures in-flight and initiate a rollback/uninstall
        that spreads the legacy behavior across engines, further spiking execution volume and triggering risk limit breaches. Finally, routing is disabled to stop new order flow,
        leaving the system in an outage state with elevated exposure still visible in risk snapshots.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 20
          component: broker_gateway
          event: |
            Market-open order flow starts; a subset of parent orders routes to the one stale SMARS engine and Power Peg warnings begin appearing.
            Execution volume rises, but routing remains enabled.
          why: |
            The stale engine still contains legacy Power Peg code and responds to a repurposed activation flag; the runaway behavior is directly observable
            via smars_router.powerpeg_warn and elevated exchange summaries. The earlier diagnostic emails exist but are not operationally acted upon.
          flows: [flow_parent_order_rlp_ok, flow_parent_order_powerpeg]
          rate_multipliers:
            flow_parent_order_rejected: 0.0
            flow_parent_order_powerpeg_widespread: 0.0
            exchange_gateway.exec_summary_spike: 0.0
            risk_monitor.limit_breach: 0.0
            config_auditor.deploy_audit_rollback: 0.0
          latency_multipliers:
            flow_parent_order_rlp_ok: {p50: 1.2, p95: 1.5}
            flow_parent_order_powerpeg: {p50: 1.2, p95: 1.6}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ops_console.triage_note
              count: 1
              hosts: [ops-01]
          manifestation:
            - smars_router.powerpeg_warn
            - exchange_gateway.exec_summary_minute
            - email_notifier.smars_diag_email
            - config_auditor.deploy_audit_partial

        - order: 2
          at_min: 30
          component: ops_console
          event: |
            Operators initiate a rollback/uninstall of the new SMARS release while the market is live.
            Power Peg-affected routing spreads across engines and exchange spike markers and backpressure become common.
          why: |
            The response action removes the working release from correctly deployed servers, increasing the population effectively running legacy behavior.
            This is reflected by a switch in deployment audit logs and by Power Peg warnings now originating from multiple engine IDs (directly observable).
          flows: [flow_parent_order_powerpeg_widespread]
          rate_multipliers:
            flow_parent_order_powerpeg: 0.0
            flow_parent_order_powerpeg_widespread: 8.0
            flow_parent_order_rlp_ok: 0.0
            exchange_gateway.exec_summary_spike: 1.0
            exchange_gateway.throttling_warn: 2.5
            risk_monitor.limit_breach: 1.0
            config_auditor.deploy_audit_partial: 0.0
            config_auditor.deploy_audit_rollback: 1.0
          latency_multipliers:
            flow_parent_order_powerpeg_widespread: {p50: 2.5, p95: 3.5}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ops_console.rollback_initiated
              count: 1
              hosts: [ops-01]
          manifestation:
            - ops_console.rollback_initiated
            - config_auditor.deploy_audit_rollback
            - smars_router.powerpeg_warn_legacy
            - exchange_gateway.exec_summary_spike
            - risk_monitor.limit_breach

        - order: 3
          at_min: 55
          component: ops_console
          event: |
            With exposure and venue backpressure still high, operators disable order routing at the broker gateway to stop new order flow.
            New parent orders are rejected; exchange spike markers subside but elevated exposure remains.
          why: |
            There is no automated kill-switch in the modeled system; the stop is achieved via an operational disable at the entry point.
            The change is observable through broker rejections and reduced spike-only exchange markers, while risk snapshots remain elevated.
          flows: [flow_parent_order_rejected]
          rate_multipliers:
            flow_parent_order_powerpeg: 0.0
            flow_parent_order_powerpeg_widespread: 0.0
            flow_parent_order_rlp_ok: 0.0
            flow_parent_order_rejected: 1.0
            exchange_gateway.exec_summary_spike: 0.0
            exchange_gateway.throttling_warn: 0.5
            email_notifier.smars_diag_email: 0.2
          latency_multipliers:
            flow_parent_order_rejected: {p50: 1.0, p95: 1.0}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ops_console.disable_routing
              count: 1
              hosts: [ops-01]
          manifestation:
            - ops_console.disable_routing
            - broker_gateway.parent_order_resp_err
            - exchange_gateway.throttling_warn
            - risk_monitor.exposure_snapshot

      steady:
        - component: broker_gateway
          condition: |
            Order routing remains disabled at the gateway; incoming parent orders are rejected.
          user_impact: |
            Broker-dealers cannot route orders through this system; order submissions receive immediate 503/429-style failures.
          manifestation: [broker_gateway.parent_order_resp_err]

        - component: risk_monitor
          condition: |
            Exposure remains elevated and requires manual unwinding; risk snapshots continue to show large net positions.
          user_impact: |
            The firm is exposed to large market risk and loss; downstream risk processes operate in degraded emergency mode.
          manifestation: [risk_monitor.exposure_snapshot, risk_monitor.limit_breach]

        - component: config_auditor
          condition: |
            Deployment audit reflects the post-rollback state (all SMARS hosts effectively stale/legacy).
          user_impact: |
            Operators lack confidence in running the router; remediation requires coordinated redeploy and verification.
          manifestation: [config_auditor.deploy_audit_rollback]

      feedback_loops:
        - id: runaway_orders_amplify_backpressure
          desc: |
            Power Peg runaway routing increases exchange submissions, which increases venue backpressure and gateway retries.
            Retries and delays add load at the broker gateway and SMARS, sustaining high traffic and worsening exposure until routing is manually disabled.

      flows: [flow_parent_order_rlp_ok, flow_parent_order_powerpeg, flow_parent_order_powerpeg_widespread, flow_parent_order_rejected]
      manifestation:
        - smars_router.powerpeg_warn
        - smars_router.powerpeg_warn_legacy
        - exchange_gateway.exec_summary_spike
        - risk_monitor.limit_breach
        - broker_gateway.parent_order_resp_err
        - config_auditor.deploy_audit_rollback

  assumptions:
    - "Time is compressed: the normal phase represents the pre-open period with diagnostic emails, and the 40-minute failure phase represents the roughly 45-minute market-open window described in the post-mortem."
    - "The rollback/uninstall spreading the legacy behavior is modeled by switching from flow_parent_order_powerpeg (engine fixed to 8) to flow_parent_order_powerpeg_widespread (engine varies 1–8) at minute 30, aligned with deploy_audit_rollback."
    - "Failure-phase piecewise breakdown (rough): 20–30 (10 min) baseline failure rates (rlp_ok=130 rpm, powerpeg=12 rpm, widespread suppressed, rejected suppressed) at ~860–900 logs/min including backgrounds; 30–55 (25 min) rollback amplification (rlp_ok suppressed, powerpeg suppressed, widespread=12 rpm × 8, exec_summary_spike enabled, throttling amplified, limit_breach enabled, deploy_audit_rollback enabled) at ~580–650 logs/min; 55–60 (5 min) routing disabled (rlp_ok/powerpeg/widespread suppressed, rejected enabled) at ~300–330 logs/min."
    - "One-shots are one triage_note at minute 20, one rollback_initiated at minute 30, and one disable_routing at minute 55."
    - "Estimated total log count is ~42k–47k logs (within 20k–100k), dominated by per-parent-order flow logs plus per-minute exchange/SMARS/risk summaries."
    - "Visibility is ensured because pre-rollback Power Peg activation produces repeated smars_router.powerpeg_warn (engine=8) and minute_stats; the rollback event produces deploy_audit_rollback plus smars_router.powerpeg_warn_legacy with varied engine IDs; risk_monitor.limit_breach and exchange_gateway.exec_summary_spike corroborate impact; broker_gateway.parent_order_resp_err marks the final outage state."
