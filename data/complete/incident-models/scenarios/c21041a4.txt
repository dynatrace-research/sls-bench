scenario:
  id: consul_tls_expiry_synchronized_restart
  title: Expired Consul TLS cert forces fleet-synchronized restart; mitigation disables date verification

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    The site serves user HTTP traffic via a frontend that relies on PostgreSQL through pgbouncer, while Patroni and pgbouncer depend on Consul for coordination.
    An expired Consul mutual-TLS certificate is initially masked by long-lived existing connections, but new connections fail on revalidation.
    Operators mitigate by staging config to disable certificate date validation and performing a synchronized Consul restart, producing brief internal Consul raft/API turbulence and delayed health-check issues.
    Customer impact is effectively unchanged (500s remain at the noise floor), but the system ends in a degraded security posture (verify_date=false).

  trigger_desc: |
    A reconnect (e.g., restart of a Consul client/agent) forces a new mTLS handshake; because the Consul certificate is expired and cannot be renewed, new connections begin failing even though existing long-lived sessions had kept working.

  time:
    total_minutes: 40
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 20}
      f: {start_min: 20, end_min: 40}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        User requests are served normally with stable DB pooling.
        Consul is healthy and largely quiet aside from low-rate raft peer snapshots, and Patroni reports Consul reachable.
        A small background of unrelated 500s exists as normal noise.
      flows: [user_http_ok_n, user_http_500_n]
      manifestation:
        - gitlab_frontend.http_access_ok
        - pgbouncer_pool.txn_ok
        - pgbouncer_pool.pool_stats
        - postgres_db.pg_stat
        - patroni_ha.patroni_loop_ok
        - consul_cluster.raft_peers

    f:
      desc: |
        The expired Consul TLS certificate becomes operationally dangerous: new or restarted clients cannot reconnect, while existing sessions can keep the site functioning.
        Operators prepare a mitigation (pause Patroni, stage config verify_date=false, schedule synchronized restarts) and then execute a fleet restart.
        During the restart, the Consul raft cluster briefly has no leader and probes observe transient internal 500/connection errors; dependent health checks may fail and can give up.
        After stabilization, service functionality returns while Consul continues operating with verify_date=false.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 20
          component: consul_cluster
          event: |
            A Consul reconnect attempt forces new TLS handshakes and Consul begins logging certificate-expiry handshake failures.
            User-facing behavior remains normal because most application/database sessions are still using existing long-lived connections.
          why: |
            Existing long-lived TCP/TLS sessions remain valid, hiding the problem until a reconnect occurs.
            The expired certificate causes x509 validation failures on new connections; the inability to renew the certificate is an external precondition.
          flows: [user_http_ok_f]

            Operators begin the mitigation: Patroni is paused, Consul config is staged with verify_date=false, Chef is stopped to avoid reverting the change,
            and monitoring probes of raft peer status are started while the restart job is scheduled.
          why: |
            The config change is safe to stage because it is only applied on restart.
            Pausing Patroni reduces risk of an unplanned failover while Consul connectivity is disrupted.
            Monitoring increases observability of raft health during the synchronized restart.
          flows: [user_http_ok_f, consul_raft_peers_probe_f]
          rate_multipliers:
            consul_raft_peers_probe_f: 1.0
          latency_multipliers: {}

            Manual restart clears health-check give-up state; the degraded posture remains because date validation is intentionally disabled.
          flows: [user_http_ok_f, consul_raft_peers_probe_f, consul_runtime_tls_config_poll_f]

              count: 1
              hosts: [db-01]

          desc: |

            Any restart or network flap forces renegotiation and can cause sudden, clustered internal failures across many services.
