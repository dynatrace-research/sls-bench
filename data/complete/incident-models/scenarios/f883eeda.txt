  title: Quay outage from App Registry traffic surge causing MySQL connection storm and lock waits

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    Quay serves registry pulls/pushes and an App Registry API. A surge of App Registry traffic drives slow,
    read-heavy code paths that hold MySQL connections longer, causing concurrent connection usage to spike
    toward the DB limit and InnoDB lock waits to appear. Clients see intermittent 502s on pulls and App
    Registry calls; pushes fail and are later forced into 503 read-only mode as SRE mitigations cap DB
    connections and disable non-essential features.

  trigger_desc: |
    A sudden increase in OperatorHub/App Registry requests (from many OpenShift clusters) hits the
    /appregistry list-packages and get-blobs endpoints, amplifying slow DB reads and inflating concurrent
    DB connection usage.

      manifestation: [mysql_rds.conn_stats_n, quay_app.worker_metric, ingress_router.http_access]

        disable namespace GC, and force read-only mode; 502s reduce but do not disappear, and pushes remain
