  title: "Kartotherian migration: pooled k8s backends unreachable + bare-metal depool triggers overload"

  summary: |
    Kartotherian serves map rendering traffic via ATS -> LVS -> backend pool. During a migration, Kubernetes workers were added to the LVS pool

    was largely avoided because ATS discarded failed origin connection attempts and kept using existing long-lived connections to bare-metal. After several
    bare-metal nodes were depooled, remaining capacity overloaded, health checks failed, and ATS returned elevated 5xx/timeouts until capacity was repooled
    and bare-metal was restarted; the underlying k8s reachability issue remained.

  trigger_desc: |
    Kubernetes workers are pooled into the Kartotherian LVS service without the LVS loopback/VIP configured on those nodes, making them unable to
    respond to health checks or successfully complete TCP/TLS connections.
