  title: EQIAD OpenSearch leader loss after rolling restart causes CirrusSearch failures until recovery and repool

    EQIAD is depooled to shift user traffic to CODFW, then the bad node is stopped and its data directory wiped so it rejoins cleanly.
    EQIAD is repooled and traffic returns to a normal split, restoring full capacity while the cluster continues to stabilize.
