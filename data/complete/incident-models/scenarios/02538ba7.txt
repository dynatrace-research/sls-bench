scenario:
  id: ingest_brownout_memory_leak_2019_11_06
  title: Intermittent ingest rejects from synchronized OOM restarts

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    The system normally accepts telemetry batches through an ALB to ingest workers, returning 202 for almost all requests.
    A slow memory leak pushes all ingest workers toward OOM at roughly the same time, causing near-synchronized restarts.
    During crash waves, some requests in flight fail (504) and some new requests cannot find reachable backends (502), leading to ~1–3% ingest rejects.
    The ingest SLO burn alert detects the brownout quickly, while a low-rate blackbox prober is intermittent and can appear to "blip" and recover.

  trigger_desc: |
    The ingest worker processes have been leaking memory for hours; at the start of the failure phase they reach OOM conditions
    within minutes of each other, producing synchronized crashes and periodic brownouts.

  time:
    total_minutes: 50
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 25}
      f: {start_min: 25, end_min: 50}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        Ingest traffic flows steadily through the ALB to three workers. Workers emit stable mem_sample logs and return 202 for
        nearly all ingests. The SLO monitor reports low error_rate_pct and the blackbox prober mostly reports successful probes.
      flows: [telemetry_ingest_accepted_n, blackbox_probe_ok_n]
      manifestation: [aws_alb.alb_access_202, ingest_worker.mem_sample, slo_monitor.ingest_slo_eval, blackbox_prober.probe_ok]

    f:
      desc: |
        Memory pressure causes all ingest workers to crash and restart within minutes of one another. This creates repeating
        windows where the ALB logs backend_unreachable (502) and backend_processing_timeout (504). Most requests still succeed,
        but intermittent rejects persist near the 1–3% level and SLO burn remains elevated. Backend-side metamonitoring summaries
        are missing during the worst crash waves, contributing to misdiagnosis.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 25
          component: ingest_worker
          event: |

          why: |
            The leak rate is similar across the worker fleet, so they hit OOM within minutes of each other. When workers exit, in-flight requests can time out (504)
            and new connections may fail (502). OOM restarts are directly observable in worker logs; the leak itself is not directly logged.
          flows: [telemetry_ingest_success_f, telemetry_ingest_unreachable_502_f, telemetry_ingest_timeout_504_f]
          rate_multipliers:
            telemetry_ingest_success_f: 0.99

            blackbox_probe_timeout_f: 1.0
          latency_multipliers:
            telemetry_ingest_success_f: {p50: 1.3, p95: 1.6}

              count: 1
              hosts: [obs-1]

              count: 1
              hosts: [obs-1]
            - ref: chatops.alert_seen
              count: 1
              hosts: [chat-1]
          manifestation: [aws_alb.alb_access_502_unreachable, aws_alb.alb_access_504_timeout, ingest_worker.oom_panic, slo_monitor.slo_burn_alert]

        - order: 2
          at_min: 33
          component: aws_alb
          event: |
            The fleet partially stabilizes after restarts and error rate drops, but intermittent 5xx persist; the blackbox prober sees enough consecutive timeouts to briefly fire.
          why: |
            Auto-restarts restore some capacity, reducing backend_unreachable and timeouts, but the underlying memory leak remains so pressure quickly returns. The blackbox probe is low rate,
            so it can miss the earlier brownout and then appear as a brief transient when consecutive runs happen to land in the failing fraction.
          flows: [blackbox_probe_timeout_f, telemetry_ingest_unreachable_502_f, telemetry_ingest_timeout_504_f]
          rate_multipliers:
            telemetry_ingest_success_f: 1.01
            telemetry_ingest_unreachable_502_f: 0.6
            telemetry_ingest_timeout_504_f: 0.6
            ingest_worker.oom_panic: 2.0
            ingest_worker.startup: 2.0
            blackbox_probe_timeout_f: 3.0
            worker_telemetry_agent.http_status_report: 0.0
          latency_multipliers:
            telemetry_ingest_success_f: {p50: 1.1, p95: 1.3}
          one_shots:
            - ref: blackbox_prober.blackbox_alert_fired
              count: 1

        - order: 3

          event: |
            A second synchronized crash wave hits, pushing 502/504 back up toward the top of the intermittent reject range while investigation focuses on ALB reachability.

          flows: [telemetry_ingest_unreachable_502_f, telemetry_ingest_timeout_504_f]

            telemetry_ingest_success_f: {p50: 1.4, p95: 1.8}

              count: 1
              hosts: [chat-1]

              count: 1
              hosts: [chat-1]

          why: |

            from worker logs and SLO diagnostics, but the underlying leaking allocation is not identified within this scenario window.
          flows: [telemetry_ingest_unreachable_502_f, telemetry_ingest_timeout_504_f]

            telemetry_ingest_success_f: 0.99
            telemetry_ingest_unreachable_502_f: 0.9

          latency_multipliers:
            telemetry_ingest_success_f: {p50: 1.3, p95: 1.6}

            - ref: chatops.hypothesis_note_backend_restarts_oom
              count: 1
              hosts: [chat-1]

      steady:
        - component: aws_alb
          condition: |
            Intermittent 502 backend_unreachable and 504 backend_processing_timeout responses continue as the worker fleet flaps.
          user_impact: |
            A small fraction (~1–3%) of customer telemetry batches are rejected at ingest during each crash wave, risking loss unless clients retry.
          manifestation: [aws_alb.alb_access_502_unreachable, aws_alb.alb_access_504_timeout]
        - component: ingest_worker
          condition: |
            Workers remain memory-pressured with recurring OOM exits and restarts; mem_sample shows high RSS.
          user_impact: |
            Increased ingest latency and occasional timeouts; capacity drops during restart windows.
          manifestation: [ingest_worker.mem_sample, ingest_worker.oom_panic, ingest_worker.startup]
        - component: slo_monitor
          condition: |
            SLO evaluation remains elevated with sustained burn relative to normal, consistent with an ongoing brownout.
          user_impact: |
            Operators see error budget burn signals even when blackbox probing is intermittent.
          manifestation: [slo_monitor.ingest_slo_eval]

      feedback_loops:
        - id: synchronized_restart_brownout
          desc: |
            Because all workers leak memory at similar rates, they approach OOM together and restart in a narrow window.
            During that window the ALB has fewer (or no) reachable backends, increasing 502/504. The reduced capacity
            concentrates load on the remaining workers, worsening latency and increasing the chance of additional timeouts.

      flows: [telemetry_ingest_success_f, telemetry_ingest_unreachable_502_f, telemetry_ingest_timeout_504_f, blackbox_probe_ok_f, blackbox_probe_timeout_f]
      manifestation: [aws_alb.alb_access_502_unreachable, aws_alb.alb_access_504_timeout, ingest_worker.oom_panic, slo_monitor.ingest_slo_eval]

  assumptions:
    - >
      Timeline is compressed to a single 25-minute brownout segment of the multi-hour incident; repeated waves are represented by
      event-driven multipliers that increase/decrease OOM restarts and ALB 5xx rates.
    - >
      Failure-phase piecewise breakdown (approx; using effective rpm after multipliers):
      minutes 25–33: ~384 success + ~6.6 (502) + ~6.6 (504) per min => ~3.3% rejects; OOM/startup boosted; worker metamonitoring suppressed.
      minutes 33–41: ~392 success + ~3.6 (502) + ~3.6 (504) per min => ~1.8% rejects; one brief blackbox alert one-shot.
      minutes 41–47: ~384 success + ~6.3 (502) + ~6.3 (504) per min => ~3.2% rejects; AWS ticket + ALB-networking hypothesis marker.
      minutes 47–50: ~384 success + ~5.4 (502) + ~5.4 (504) per min => ~2.7% rejects; hypothesis marker shifts toward OOM evidence.
    - >
      Rough effective volume:
      normal ~25 min * ~812 logs/min ≈ 20k logs;
      failure ~25 min * ~800 logs/min plus restart spikes/one-shots ≈ 21k logs;
      total ≈ 41k logs (within 20k–100k).
    - >
      Visibility rationale: ALB 502/504 access logs appear reliably due to high ingest rpm even at 1–3% rates; OOM/restart background logs
      are boosted during crash-wave events; SLO eval emits once per minute throughout, and key alerts/diagnostics are anchored with one-shots
      so phase boundaries are clear even if blackbox probing is sparse. ChatOps hypothesis markers are fixed-value templates, preventing
      contradictory one-shot content relative to the event narrative.
