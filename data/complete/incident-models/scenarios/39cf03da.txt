  title: Telia transit packet loss causes origin timeouts (522) until manual drain

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    The CDN edge normally serves most requests from cache and fetches the rest from customer origins through multiple transit providers.
    During the incident, network monitoring detected severe packet loss on the Telia transit backbone, and origin connection attempts over that path timed out.
    This manifested as a spike in HTTP 522 errors returned by the edge (origin unreachable) and elevated end-user latency.
    Engineers manually drained/disabled Telia ports to reroute traffic to other providers, which removed Telia loss signals but left residual 522s and congestion symptoms.

  trigger_desc: |
    Network path monitoring detects massive packet loss on Telia transit paths used for origin-bound traffic; no automatic mitigation is applied for the affected large PoPs, so traffic continues to use Telia until an operator drains the provider.
