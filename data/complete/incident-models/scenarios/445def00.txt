scenario:
  id: shard4_xid_wraparound_degradation
  title: Shard4 XID wraparound protection causes email send degradation; truncation enables vacuum completion and recovery

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    Mandrill executes send jobs by writing per-message/job state into a sharded Postgres K/V store and then delivering via workers.
    A hot shard (shard4) hits the Postgres transaction ID (XID) wraparound protection threshold and enters safety shutdown/maintenance,
    rejecting writes. Shard4-mapped jobs begin failing with retries and disk spooling, reducing effective delivery to about 80% while disk pressure
    and log-aggregation backlog grow. Responders run a manual VACUUM and then truncate the largest tables to unblock progress; VACUUM completes,
    shard4 becomes writable again, and backlog drain begins, though residual queues/disk pressure remain at the end of the modeled window.

  trigger_desc: |
    Shard4's XID age reaches the wraparound protection limit due to autovacuum lag on a hot shard, causing Postgres to shut down/refuse writes.

  time:
    total_minutes: 50
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 25}
      f: {start_min: 25, end_min: 50}

      desc: |
        Send traffic is steady. Most jobs map to non-shard4 shards and complete normally; a smaller portion maps to shard4 and also completes.
        Background metrics show healthy queue depths, modest disk utilization, and routine Postgres checkpoints and XID age telemetry.
      flows: [send_email_other, send_email_shard4_ok]
      manifestation:
        - mandrill_api.healthcheck
        - mandrill_api.disk_usage_metric
        - mail_worker.queue_depth
        - postgres_shard4.xid_metric
        - log_aggregator.ingest_summary

        centralized error reporting, then start shard4 maintenance in standalone mode with a manual VACUUM. When VACUUM proves too slow, responders
        truncate the largest tables to reduce workload; this accelerates progress, VACUUM completes, and shard4 returns to read-write so sending from
        backlog resumes and backlogs begin draining.
