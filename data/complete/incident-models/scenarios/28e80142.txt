```yaml
scenario:
  id: beagle_slo_crashloop_due_to_topic_typo
  title: Beagle crash-loop and SLO processing delay after mis-typed Kafka topic in runbook

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    Retriever continuously publishes mutation events to Kafka and Beagle consumes them to compute SLO points.
    During a retriever scale-up, operators apply an SLO definition referencing a Kafka topic, but the topic name
    contains an underscore instead of a hyphen. Beagle repeatedly fails to start consuming that non-existent topic,
    crash-loops, and SLO processing falls behind, leading to customer-visible SLO data delay.

  trigger_desc: |
    An operator applies a runbook step that inserts/updates a Beagle SLO definition using the mis-typed topic
    "honeycomb-prod.retriever_mutation" (underscore), while producers use "honeycomb-prod.retriever-mutation" (hyphen).

  time:
    total_minutes: 40
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 18}
      f: {start_min: 18, end_min: 40}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        Steady ingest and SLO processing. Retriever publishes to the correct Kafka mutation topic and Beagle keeps up.
        Beagle lag metrics remain low and there are only occasional consumer rebalances.
      flows: [publish_retriever_mutation, beagle_process_slo_batch]
      manifestation: [retriever.mutation_publish_ok, beagle.batch_processed, beagle.lag_metric, alerting.notifier_ok]

    f:
      desc: |
        A runbook introduces an SLO definition pointing to a mis-typed Kafka topic. Beagle repeatedly fails to consume
        that topic and enters a restart loop; normal batch processing stops and lag cannot be reliably reported
        from Beagle while it is not stably running. Operators attempt to mitigate by scaling/changing Beagle, which
        increases consumer-group churn but does not resolve the underlying topic mismatch. After the typo is found and
        corrected, Beagle resumes batch processing but remains significantly lagged and continues draining backlog
        slowly through the end of the scenario.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 18
          component: ops_tool
          event: |
            During retriever scale-up, an SLO definition is applied with a mis-typed topic string. Beagle begins failing
            to start its consumer and enters a restart loop; alerts begin firing.
          why: |
            Beagle reads SLO topic definitions from the SLO database; the mis-typed topic does not exist in Kafka, so
            consumer startup fails with UnknownTopicOrPartition and Beagle exits. This is directly observable via the
            ops_tool apply log plus Beagle's unknown-topic and restart/exit logs; the causal link requires knowing that
            Beagle uses those SLO definitions to subscribe.
          flows: [publish_retriever_mutation, beagle_consume_unknown_topic]
          rate_multipliers:
            beagle_process_slo_batch: 0.0
            beagle_consume_unknown_topic: 1.0
            beagle.process_start: 4.0
            beagle.lag_metric: 0.0
            alerting.alert_fired: 1.0
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ops_tool.scale_retriever
              count: 1
              hosts: [ops-1]
            - ref: ops_tool.apply_slo_def_wrong
              count: 1
              hosts: [ops-1]
          manifestation:
            - ops_tool.apply_slo_def_wrong
            - beagle.consumer_error_unknown_topic
            - beagle.panic_exit
            - beagle.process_start
            - alerting.alert_fired

        - order: 2
          at_min: 26
          component: ops_tool
          event: |
            Operators attempt to mitigate by scaling/changing Beagle, causing more consumer group churn and more frequent
            restarts, but consumption still fails due to the same topic mismatch.
          why: |
            Changing Beagle's runtime characteristics (replicas/workers) triggers consumer rebalances and restarts, but
            does not fix the invalid topic configuration. This is observable via increased rebalance and process_start
            logs plus continued UnknownTopicOrPartition errors; lag logs remain absent while Beagle is crash-looping.
          flows: [beagle_consume_unknown_topic]
          rate_multipliers:
            beagle_consume_unknown_topic: 2.0
            beagle.process_start: 8.0
            beagle.consumer_rebalance: 6.0
            beagle.lag_metric: 0.0
            alerting.alert_fired: 1.5
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ops_tool.scale_beagle
              count: 1
              hosts: [ops-1]
          manifestation:
            - ops_tool.scale_beagle
            - beagle.consumer_rebalance
            - beagle.consumer_error_unknown_topic
            - beagle.process_start
            - alerting.alert_fired

        - order: 3
          at_min: 34
          component: ops_tool
          event: |
            The topic typo is identified and the SLO definition is corrected to the hyphenated topic. Beagle stops
            emitting unknown-topic errors and resumes batch processing, but remains behind and drains lag slowly.
          why: |
            With the correct topic configured, Beagle can successfully subscribe and process batches again. The end of
            UnknownTopicOrPartition errors is directly observable; persistent lag is observable via lag_metric and the
            high-lag values in batch_processed.
          flows: [beagle_process_slo_batch]
          rate_multipliers:
            beagle_consume_unknown_topic: 0.0
            beagle_process_slo_batch: 1.0
            beagle.process_start: 0.1
            beagle.consumer_rebalance: 2.0
            beagle.lag_metric: 1.0
            alerting.alert_fired: 1.0
          latency_multipliers:
            beagle_process_slo_batch: {p50: 1.1, p95: 1.2}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ops_tool.apply_slo_def_correct
              count: 1
              hosts: [ops-1]
          manifestation:
            - ops_tool.apply_slo_def_correct
            - beagle.batch_processed
            - beagle.lag_metric
            - alerting.alert_fired

      steady:
        - component: beagle
          condition: |
            Beagle is processing again but is substantially behind; p95 consumer lag remains in the minutes range
            through the end of the scenario. Rebalances occur occasionally while backlog drains.
          user_impact: |
            SLO-derived data (dashboards/alerts depending on SLO processing) is delayed and appears stale; some customers
            see several minutes (or more) of delay in SLO computations.
          manifestation: [beagle.lag_metric, beagle.batch_processed, alerting.alert_fired]

      feedback_loops:
        - id: crashloop_blocks_catchup
          desc: |
            While Beagle is crash-looping, offsets do not advance, so lag grows. Larger lag increases operational pressure
            and prompts scaling/restarts that can further increase rebalances, slowing stabilization even after fixes.

      flows: [publish_retriever_mutation, beagle_consume_unknown_topic, beagle_process_slo_batch]
      manifestation:
        - ops_tool.apply_slo_def_wrong
        - ops_tool.apply_slo_def_correct
        - beagle.consumer_error_unknown_topic
        - beagle.panic_exit
        - beagle.process_start
        - beagle.lag_metric
        - alerting.alert_fired

  assumptions:
    - "Timeline is compressed into a 40-minute window: 18 minutes normal, then a 22-minute failure that ends while still lagged (no full recovery modeled)."
    - |
      Failure-phase piecewise behavior and approximate effective rates (order-of-magnitude, after multipliers):
      - min 18-26 (8 min): beagle_process_slo_batch suppressed (0.0); beagle_consume_unknown_topic at 1.0x (30 rpm => ~60 logs/min);
        beagle.process_start at 4.0x (7.5/min => ~30 logs/min); beagle.lag_metric suppressed (0.0); alerts active.
        Effective logs/min ≈ ~2.1k (dominated by retriever publishes).
      - min 26-34 (8 min): unknown-topic attempts increase to 2.0x (60 rpm => ~120 logs/min); beagle.process_start increases to 8.0x (~60 logs/min);
        rebalances increase; beagle.lag_metric remains suppressed (0.0). Effective logs/min ≈ ~2.2k.
      - min 34-40 (6 min): unknown-topic flow suppressed (0.0); beagle_process_slo_batch resumes at 1.0x (30 rpm => ~90 logs/min) with high lag;
        beagle.lag_metric resumes (1.0x); restarts drop sharply (process_start 0.1x => <1/min). Effective logs/min ≈ ~2.1k.
    - "One-shots per event: event1 has 2 (scale_retriever, apply_slo_def_wrong); event2 has 1 (scale_beagle); event3 has 1 (apply_slo_def_correct)."
    - "Backlog plausibility: crash-looping prevents offset advancement for ~16 minutes (min 18-34), building a backlog of ~32k mutation events at 2000/min; post-fix processing is modeled to catch up only modestly faster than production, so minutes of lag persist through min 40."
    - "Volume estimate: normal ~40k logs (18 min * ~2247 logs/min) + failure ~46k–50k logs (22 min at ~2.1k–2.2k logs/min depending on interval) ≈ ~86k–90k total, within the 20k–100k target."
    - "Crash-loop visibility is ensured by aligned beagle.process_start (~30/min then ~60/min) plus beagle_consume_unknown_topic errors/exits at the same order; lag visibility resumes after the fix via beagle.lag_metric and high-lag beagle.batch_processed."
```
