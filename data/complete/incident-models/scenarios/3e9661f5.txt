scenario:
  id: filestore_global_read_throttle_2022_09_13
  title: Global read-only API outage due to internal overload and global throttling

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    The Filestore control plane normally serves read-only queries (ListInstances, GetOperation) and mutate requests
    that create long-running operations. An internal project-management service malfunctions and floods the read-only
    APIs, pushing Filestore over a global read quota and causing system-wide 429s on read-only calls. Mutate operations
    still return operations, but customers cannot poll operation status, degrading usability and observability.

  trigger_desc: |
    An internal service that scans many GCP projects enters an abnormal rapid reconcile loop, issuing excessive
    ListInstances calls and causing the Filestore global read quota gate to throttle all read-only requests.

  time:
    total_minutes: 50
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 20}
      f: {start_min: 20, end_min: 50}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        Customer console/gcloud/API read-only calls succeed with low latency, and operation polling returns status from
        the operations manager. Mutate calls (e.g., CreateInstance) pass the global quota gate and return 202 with an
        operation id. The internal project-management sweeper runs at low rate and stays below the global read limit.
      flows: [list_instances_ok, get_operation_ok, create_instance_mutate, internal_project_sync_ok]
      manifestation:
        - filestore_api.access_list_instances_ok
        - filestore_api.access_get_operation_ok
        - global_quota.quota_metrics
        - monitoring.synthetic_check_ok

    f:
      desc: |
        A sudden spike in internal read-only traffic pushes the system above a global read quota, and the throttling
        gate rejects read-only API calls with 429 across all customers. Operation polling (GetOperation) is also read-only,
        so customers cannot check the progress of otherwise-successful mutate operations. Engineers detect the issue via
        monitoring and begin mitigation by pausing the internal service, reducing one major load source but leaving throttling still effectively
        denying read-only requests by the end of this scenario slice.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 20
          component: internal_project_manager
          event: |
            The internal project-management service enters a rapid reconcile loop, sharply increasing ListInstances traffic.
            Global read throttling is enforced and read-only calls start returning 429.
          why: |
            Filestore enforces a global read request limit; the internal service's abnormal request flood pushes observed read demand
            above the limit, causing the throttling gate to reject read-only requests. The internal service malfunction is directly
            observable via its marker log; the global throttling is directly observable via quota metrics, throttle-gate warnings,
            and API 429 access logs.
          flows: [internal_project_sync_429, list_instances_429, get_operation_429, create_instance_mutate]
          rate_multipliers: {}
          latency_multipliers:
            create_instance_mutate: {p50: 1.5, p95: 2.0}
          one_shots:
            - ref: internal_project_manager.rapid_reconcile_entered
              count: 1
              hosts: [proj-mgr-1]
          manifestation:
            - internal_project_manager.rapid_reconcile_entered
            - global_quota.throttle_gate_open
            - filestore_api.access_list_instances_429
            - filestore_api.access_get_operation_429

        - order: 2
          at_min: 27
          component: monitoring
          event: |
            Monitoring pages on-call for sustained global read throttling and synthetic read-only probe failures.
          why: |
            Synthetic ListInstances checks observe 429s and quota metrics remain over limit, triggering the alert rule.
            This is directly observable through the synthetic check failures and the alert marker.
          flows: [list_instances_429, internal_project_sync_429]
          rate_multipliers: {}
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots:
            - ref: monitoring.alert_throttle_high
              count: 1
              hosts: [mon-1]
          manifestation:
            - monitoring.alert_throttle_high
            - monitoring.synthetic_check_fail_429
            - global_quota.quota_metrics

        - order: 3
          at_min: 45
          component: internal_project_manager
          event: |
            Engineers begin mitigation by pausing the internal project-management service, reducing internal read-only request volume.
            Global throttling remains active and customer read-only calls continue to be denied (still observed as 429s).
          why: |
            Pausing the internal source reduces the internal contribution to global read pressure (directly observable via the internal
            source's operator-action log and reduced internal sweep activity). However, the global read gate remains active and continues
            rejecting read-only calls, which remains directly observable via persistent 429 access logs and ongoing synthetic failures.
          flows: [internal_project_sync_429, list_instances_429, get_operation_429]
          rate_multipliers:
            internal_project_sync_429: 0.3
            internal_project_manager.sweep_cycle: 0.3
            global_quota.throttle_gate_open: 0.6
          latency_multipliers:
            create_instance_mutate: {p50: 1.2, p95: 1.5}
          one_shots:
            - ref: internal_project_manager.pause_internal_service
              count: 1
              hosts: [proj-mgr-1]
          manifestation:
            - internal_project_manager.pause_internal_service
            - global_quota.quota_metrics
            - filestore_api.access_list_instances_429

      steady:
        - component: global_quota
          condition: |
            Global read throttling remains active; read-only requests are still denied at the global gate (near-total throttling).
          user_impact: |
            Customers in all locations experience read-only Filestore control-plane calls failing (e.g., listing instances and polling operations).
          manifestation:
            - global_quota.throttle_gate_open
            - global_quota.quota_metrics

        - component: filestore_api
          condition: |
            Read-only API methods return 429 quickly, while mutate methods continue to return 202 with operation ids.
          user_impact: |
            Console/gcloud/API cannot perform read-only inventory queries; operation status polling fails, reducing visibility into ongoing mutate work.
          manifestation:
            - filestore_api.access_list_instances_429
            - filestore_api.access_get_operation_429
            - filestore_api.access_create_instance_accepted

        - component: monitoring
          condition: |
            Synthetic read-only probes keep failing with 429, reflecting ongoing customer impact.
          user_impact: |
            Operators continue to observe the issue via synthetic failures while customers remain throttled.
          manifestation:
            - monitoring.synthetic_check_fail_429

      feedback_loops:
        - id: overload_throttle_retry_loop
          desc: |
            As throttling rejects read-only calls, clients and internal automation tend to repeat inventory/polling calls,
            sustaining elevated observed read traffic and keeping the global throttle gate open.

      flows: [list_instances_429, get_operation_429, create_instance_mutate, internal_project_sync_429]
      manifestation:
        - filestore_api.access_list_instances_429
        - filestore_api.access_get_operation_429
        - global_quota.quota_metrics
        - global_quota.throttle_gate_open
        - monitoring.synthetic_check_fail_429

  assumptions:
    - |
      The real incident lasted hours and ended in full mitigation; this scenario compresses it to a 50-minute slice and ends
      while still failing to satisfy the "end in failure" requirement.
    - |
      quota_metrics is treated as an aggregated/rolling summary that may include additional unmodeled read-only RPCs and does not necessarily
      drop immediately when one load source is reduced; this keeps the metric consistent with the modeled high 429 volume without requiring
      hidden coupling at per-request granularity.
    - |
      Piecewise failure breakdown (30 minutes total failure):
      - Minutes 20-44 (25 min): internal_project_sync_429 at 1500 rpm; customer list/get traffic continues; global_quota quota_metrics at 1/min and
        throttle_gate_open at 1/min; internal_project_manager sweep_cycle at 6/min per host.
      - Minutes 45-49 (5 min): internal_project_sync_429 reduced to ~450 rpm (multiplier 0.3); internal_project_manager sweep_cycle reduced to
        ~1.8/min per host (multiplier 0.3); throttle_gate_open reduced to ~0.6/min; read-only 429s persist.
      One-shots: rapid_reconcile_entered (1), alert_throttle_high (1), pause_internal_service (1).
    - |
      Coherency note: after mitigation, the internal sweep summary rate (~3.6 sweep logs/min total across 2 hosts) with api_calls 110-150
      supports roughly 396-540 calls/min, which is consistent with the reduced internal request flow (~450 rpm).
    - |
      Rough volume check (including the mitigation reduction after minute 45):
      - Normal (20 min): ~810 flow-logs/min => ~16.2k flow logs; plus ~160 background logs => ~16.36k.
      - Failure (25 min at ~2060 logs/min + 5 min at ~1000 logs/min): ~56.5k logs; plus 3 one-shots => ~56.5k.
      Total ~72.9k logs, ensuring high likelihood of seeing API 429s, quota metrics, synthetic failures, and the operator/alert one-shots.
