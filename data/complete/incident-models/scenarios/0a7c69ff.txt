    Direct Connect normally forwards private customer traffic from edge POPs to the Tokyo region with near-zero loss.

    causing elevated packet loss and occasional blackholing for traffic destined to ap-northeast-1.
    Automation detects an abnormally high device failure rate and defers its normal auto-isolation behavior, forcing
    manual mitigations that only partially relieve the impact. Late in the window, engineers disable a fast-convergence
    protocol in a single AZ (AZ2), and logs show AZ2 traffic switching to a legacy convergence mode with partial improvement.

  trigger_desc: |
    Customer traffic patterns in the Tokyo Direct Connect network hit a rare packet attribute combination that interacts
    poorly with a fast-convergence protocol on a subset of transport devices, leading to intermittent forwarding failures.
