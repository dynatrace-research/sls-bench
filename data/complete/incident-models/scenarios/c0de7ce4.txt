  id: incident_2022_11_18_heroku_h10_poison_pubsub_welcome
  title: Crash-loop downtime from poison Pub/Sub welcome message and uncaught goroutine panic

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    The incident app normally serves interactive HTTP traffic and consumes Pub/Sub work with near-zero backlog.
    A rare welcome-message Pub/Sub delivery triggers an unhandled panic inside a goroutine created by the Pub/Sub client,
    crashing the Heroku dyno and causing intermittent H10/503 downtime. Large panic output causes Logplex to drop and later
    rate-limit logs, slowing diagnosis. Responders mitigate by purging suspect subscriptions and disabling non-critical
    async-producing features, restoring stability but leaving best-effort async behavior degraded.

  trigger_desc: |
    A poison message in the inc-welcome-sub subscription is delivered and processed by a handler that panics in a
    child goroutine without a recover(), causing the whole Go process to exit and Heroku to report H10 "App crashed".

      manifestation:
