    The DB cluster manager misinterprets coordination signals, promotes a primary, and (due to a defect) begins a full replica rebuild, leaving the primary without replicas and overloaded.
    API success rate drops to ~75% at times and the dashboard becomes extremely slow when reads are temporarily routed to the DR replica; the scenario ends before replicas are rebuilt.

  trigger_desc: |
    A top-of-rack switch enters a partial failure state where some packets are not forwarded even though link aggregation stays up, creating inconsistent connectivity between one etcd member and the leader.
