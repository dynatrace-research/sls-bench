scenario:
  id: leap_second_negative_srtt_rrdns
  title: Leap-second clock rewind triggers recovered panics in CNAME DNS resolution

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    The edge DNS service normally answers authoritative queries and performs internal recursive lookups only for CNAME-based origin discovery.
    During the leap second, a subset of hosts experienced a ~1s backward clock step, allowing negative wall-clock RTT samples to enter resolver performance smoothing.
    Within minutes, some resolver-selection weights became invalid and triggered recovered panics during CNAME lookup selection, returning SERVFAIL for a small fraction of DNS queries.
    A hotfix rollout started with a canary restart and reduced errors, but the scenario ends while at least one affected host remains unpatched and a small residual SERVFAIL rate persists.

  trigger_desc: |
    A leap-second-related time synchronization adjustment steps the system clock backward on a subset of edge hosts while they are measuring resolver RTTs using a non-monotonic wall clock.

  time:
    total_minutes: 56
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 28}
      f: {start_min: 28, end_min: 56}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        Edge DNS hosts serve mostly healthy query traffic. A minority of queries require resolving a customer-configured CNAME
        target to discover origin IPs, which triggers internal recursive DNS activity and weighted selection among internal resolvers.
        Periodic metrics show stable smoothed RTTs and essentially no panics.
      flows: [dns_simple_query_n, dns_cname_lookup_n, dns_cname_lookup_retry_n]
      manifestation:
        - dns_edge.dns_response_ok
        - dns_edge.upstream_selected
        - internal_resolver.recursive_response_ok
        - dns_edge.rrdns_metrics

    f:
      desc: |
        After the leap-second clock rewind on a subset of edge hosts, some resolver RTT measurements become negative.
        Due to smoothing, the smoothed RTT used for weighting can drift below zero after enough samples.
        When a CNAME lookup on an affected host tries to perform weighted upstream selection, an invalid (negative) weight can trigger a panic;
        the panic is recovered, but the DNS request returns SERVFAIL. A hotfix later resets negative stats on patched hosts and rolling
        restarts reduce the impacted fraction, but not all affected hosts are restarted within this scenario window.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 28
          component: time_sync
          event: |
            A leap-second adjustment steps the clock backward on two edge hosts; initial CNAME lookups on those hosts begin to intermittently fail with recovered panics and SERVFAIL.
          why: |
            RRDNS measures resolver RTT with wall-clock time.Now(); a backward step can produce negative RTT samples.
            Those samples can later influence smoothed stats and the weighted selection code path. The clock step is directly observable via time-sync logs;
            the internal negative RTT/smoothed state is only indirectly observable through resulting panics and (later) anomaly metrics.
          flows: [dns_cname_panic_fail_f]
          rate_multipliers:
            dns_cname_fixed_reset_f: 0.0
            dns_edge.rrdns_metrics_neg: 0.0
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots:
            - ref: time_sync.clock_step
              count: 2
              hosts: [dns-edge-02, dns-edge-04]
          manifestation:
            - time_sync.clock_step
            - dns_edge.rrdns_panic_recovered
            - dns_edge.dns_response_servfail

        - order: 2
          at_min: 32
          component: monitoring
          event: |
            Monitoring detects elevated SERVFAIL for CNAME-related queries and opens a paging alert for DNS errors.
          why: |
            The SERVFAIL responses generated after recovered panics raise the observed error-rate metric above the alert threshold.
            This alert is directly observable in monitoring logs, but it is an effect rather than the root cause.
          flows: [dns_cname_panic_fail_f]
          rate_multipliers: {}
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots:
            - ref: monitoring.alert_open
              count: 1
              hosts: [mon-01]
          manifestation:
            - monitoring.alert_open
            - dns_edge.dns_response_servfail

        - order: 3
          at_min: 38
          component: dns_edge
          event: |
            As smoothing incorporates additional negative RTT samples, recovered panics become more frequent and hosts begin emitting anomaly metrics that show negative min_srtt_ms.
          why: |
            Smoothing can accumulate enough negative contribution that per-resolver smoothed RTT becomes negative.
            When that negative value reaches the weighted-selection routine, it can trigger a panic (recovered), failing the request.
            This progression is observable via increased recovered-panic logs and the onset of rrdns_metrics_neg anomaly snapshots.
          flows: [dns_cname_panic_fail_f]
          rate_multipliers:
            dns_cname_panic_fail_f: 4.0
            dns_edge.rrdns_metrics: 1.4
            dns_edge.rrdns_metrics_neg: 1.0
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots: []
          manifestation:
            - dns_edge.rrdns_metrics_neg
            - dns_edge.rrdns_panic_recovered
            - dns_edge.dns_response_servfail

        - order: 4
          at_min: 44
          component: deploy_orchestrator
          event: |
            A hotfix is deployed to a canary node and that node is restarted; on patched nodes, negative upstream stats are reset instead of propagating into selection, reducing SERVFAIL and reducing anomaly-metric frequency.
          why: |
            The hotfix prevents negative values from reaching weighted selection by resetting upstream performance state when a negative stat is detected.
            Restarting clears any already-corrupted in-memory state. The deployment and restart are directly observable; the behavioral change is observable through reset logs, fewer panics, and fewer anomaly snapshots.
          flows: [dns_cname_fixed_reset_f, dns_cname_panic_fail_f]
          rate_multipliers:
            dns_cname_panic_fail_f: 1.5
            dns_cname_fixed_reset_f: 1.0
            dns_edge.rrdns_metrics: 1.0
            dns_edge.rrdns_metrics_neg: 0.6
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots:
            - ref: deploy_orchestrator.deploy_start
              count: 1
              hosts: [deploy-01]
            - ref: dns_edge.process_restart
              count: 1
              hosts: [dns-edge-02]
            - ref: deploy_orchestrator.deploy_finish
              count: 1
              hosts: [deploy-01]
          manifestation:
            - deploy_orchestrator.deploy_start
            - dns_edge.process_restart
            - dns_edge.upstream_stats_reset

        - order: 5
          at_min: 50
          component: deploy_orchestrator
          event: |
            Rollout expands to additional edge hosts; recovered panics, SERVFAIL, and negative-sRTT anomaly snapshots continue to fall, but an affected host has not yet been restarted and a small residual failure rate remains by the end of the window.
          why: |
            As more hosts are patched/restarted, fewer queries land on instances with corrupted in-memory stats.
            Residual errors indicate at least one clock-stepped host is still running with corrupted state (or without the hotfix) within this compressed scenario window.
          flows: [dns_cname_fixed_reset_f, dns_cname_panic_fail_f]
          rate_multipliers:
            dns_cname_panic_fail_f: 0.6
            dns_cname_fixed_reset_f: 2.0
            dns_edge.rrdns_metrics: 0.8
            dns_edge.rrdns_metrics_neg: 0.3
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots:
            - ref: deploy_orchestrator.deploy_start
              count: 1
              hosts: [deploy-01]
            - ref: dns_edge.process_restart
              count: 1
              hosts: [dns-edge-03]
            - ref: deploy_orchestrator.deploy_finish
              count: 1
              hosts: [deploy-01]
          manifestation:
            - deploy_orchestrator.deploy_finish
            - dns_edge.rrdns_panic_recovered
            - dns_edge.dns_response_servfail

      steady:
        - component: dns_edge
          condition: |
            A small fraction of CNAME lookups still hit an affected edge host that has not yet been restarted/patched (e.g., dns-edge-04),
            where negative smoothed RTT state can trigger recovered panics during weighted resolver selection.
          user_impact: |
            Some DNS resolutions for CNAME-based configurations intermittently return SERVFAIL; a smaller downstream fraction of HTTP requests would fail due to unsuccessful name resolution.
          manifestation:
            - dns_edge.rrdns_panic_recovered
            - dns_edge.dns_response_servfail
            - dns_edge.rrdns_metrics_neg

      feedback_loops:
        - id: smoothed_negative_srtt_persists
          desc: |
            Once negative RTT samples are incorporated into smoothed upstream performance, the negative state persists on that host and affects subsequent selections.
            This creates repeated panics on that instance until the stats are reset by hotfix logic or cleared by a restart.

      flows:
        - dns_simple_query_f
        - dns_cname_lookup_f
        - dns_cname_lookup_retry_f
        - dns_cname_panic_fail_f
        - dns_cname_fixed_reset_f
      manifestation:
        - dns_edge.rrdns_panic_recovered
        - dns_edge.dns_response_servfail
        - dns_edge.rrdns_metrics
        - dns_edge.rrdns_metrics_neg
        - dns_edge.upstream_stats_reset

  assumptions:
    - >
      The original incident lasted multiple hours across many data centers; this scenario compresses the timeline to 56 minutes while keeping the ordering:
      clock rewind → smoothing-driven worsening (now evidenced by delayed onset of rrdns_metrics_neg) → canary hotfix → broader rollout (still incomplete by end).
    - >
      Failure-phase piecewise breakdown (effective): minutes 28-38 low errors (panic flow ~0.4 rpm; rrdns_metrics_neg suppressed);
      minutes 38-44 peak (panic flow ~1.6 rpm; rrdns_metrics_neg enabled); minutes 44-50 mitigated (panic flow ~0.6 rpm; fixed-reset flow ~2 rpm; anomaly metrics reduced);
      minutes 50-56 further reduced but not eliminated (panic flow ~0.24 rpm; fixed-reset flow ~4 rpm; anomaly metrics rarer).
    - >
      Approximate log volume: normal phase 28 min * ~1330 logs/min ≈ 37k; failure phase 28 min * ~1330–1345 logs/min plus one-shots ≈ 37k–38k;
      total ≈ 74k–75k logs (within 20k–100k).
    - >
      Visibility rationale: the trigger is evidenced by time_sync.clock_step one-shots; the smoothing-driven progression is evidenced by the onset of dns_edge.rrdns_metrics_neg
      plus increased dns_edge.rrdns_panic_recovered and dns_edge.dns_response_servfail; mitigation/rollout is evidenced by deploy_orchestrator logs, dns_edge.process_restart,
      and dns_edge.upstream_stats_reset after patch activation.
    - >
      Rollout is intentionally modeled as incomplete: only one of the two clock-stepped edge hosts is restarted early in-window, leaving a supported residual dns_cname_panic_fail_f rate in steady state.
