scenario:
  id: useast_2011_ebs_remirror_storm_control_plane_brownout
  title: Misrouted AZ network change triggers EBS re-mirroring storm and control plane brownouts

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    EC2 instances in one AZ rely on EBS for block I/O, and customers use a regional EBS control plane for volume APIs.
    A network scaling change in the AZ mistakenly routes primary EBS traffic onto a lower-capacity replication network,
    isolating many EBS nodes and triggering a re-mirroring storm with capacity exhaustion. Volumes become "stuck",
    EC2/RDS observe blocked I/O, and the control plane experiences thread pool saturation that briefly elevates API
    errors/latencies across the region until mitigations isolate the degraded cluster.

  trigger_desc: |
    A planned network capacity upgrade shifts traffic off a redundant router, but the traffic is misrouted onto the
    replication network, simultaneously breaking primary and secondary connectivity for many EBS nodes in one AZ.

  time:
    total_minutes: 50
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 20}
      f: {start_min: 20, end_min: 50}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        Steady-state operation: EC2 hosts perform regular read/write I/O to EBS volumes with low latency, customers
        create and attach volumes via the EBS control plane, and the EBS cluster occasionally runs low-rate primary
        elections for normal maintenance. Metrics show low stuck volume percentage and minimal control plane errors.
      flows: [ec2_volume_io_ok, ebs_api_create_volume_ok, ebs_api_attach_volume_ok, ebs_elect_primary_ok, rds_txn_ok]
      manifestation:
        - ebs_node_cluster.cluster_health
        - ebs_control_plane.cp_metrics
        - ebs_node_cluster.io_complete
        - rds_service.txn_commit

    f:
      desc: |
        After the network misroute, many EBS nodes lose replica connectivity and aggressively attempt re-mirroring.
        Limited free capacity causes repeated capacity searches and a surge of primary election traffic, blocking
        access to some volumes and making EC2/RDS I/O appear stuck. Long-running CreateVolume calls and elevated
        internal election traffic contribute to control plane thread pool saturation that intermittently degrades
        EBS APIs beyond the affected AZ. Operators mitigate by disabling CreateVolume in the affected AZ (turning
        slow timeouts into fast rejects), then later blocking control-plane communication to the degraded cluster to
        protect the rest of the region, leaving the affected AZ inaccessible with a persistent set of stuck volumes.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 20
          component: net_ops
          event: |
            A network change is applied and traffic is misrouted to the replication network; rollback completes but
            many nodes have already been isolated, triggering widespread re-mirroring searches and initial stuck I/O.
          why: |
            With both primary and effective secondary connectivity disrupted, nodes assume replicas failed and begin
            re-mirroring. After connectivity returns, concurrent demand for new replicas quickly exhausts free capacity,
            causing repeated searches and blocking I/O until a writable primary can be elected (directly observable via
            remirror_activity/cluster_health and blocked I/O logs).
          flows:
            - ec2_volume_io_stuck
            - ebs_api_create_volume_timeout
            - ebs_elect_primary_ok_load
          rate_multipliers:
            ebs_api_create_volume_disabled_fast: 0.0
            ebs_control_plane.partition_status: 0.0
            ebs_control_plane.thread_pool_saturated: 0.8
            ebs_elect_primary_err_blocked: 0.0
          latency_multipliers:
            ebs_api_create_volume_timeout: {p50: 1.2, p95: 1.2}
          one_shots:
            - ref: net_ops.change_start
              count: 1
              hosts: [netops-1]
            - ref: net_ops.misroute_detected
              count: 1
              hosts: [netops-1]
            - ref: net_ops.rollback_complete
              count: 1
              hosts: [netops-1]
          manifestation:
            - net_ops.misroute_detected
            - ebs_node_cluster.remirror_activity
            - ebs_node_cluster.io_blocked_remirror
            - ebs_control_plane.thread_pool_saturated

        - order: 2
          at_min: 28
          component: ebs_control_plane
          event: |
            Operators disable new CreateVolume requests in the affected AZ, shifting CreateVolume behavior from long
            upstream timeouts to fast rejection; regional API behavior partially improves.
          why: |
            Long upstream timeouts from the degraded cluster cause queued work and thread starvation. Rejecting new
            CreateVolume work immediately prevents those long holds on the shared thread pool, improving the control plane's
            ability to handle other requests (observable via reduced saturation and improved metrics).
          flows:
            - ebs_api_create_volume_disabled_fast
            - ebs_api_attach_volume_ok_slow
            - ebs_api_attach_volume_err
          rate_multipliers:
            ebs_api_create_volume_timeout: 0.0
            ebs_api_create_volume_disabled_fast: 1.0
            ebs_control_plane.thread_pool_saturated: 0.2
            ebs_api_attach_volume_err: 0.3
          latency_multipliers:
            ebs_api_attach_volume_ok_slow: {p50: 0.7, p95: 0.7}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ebs_control_plane.create_volume_disabled
              count: 1
              hosts: [ebs-cp-01]
          manifestation:
            - ebs_control_plane.create_volume_disabled
            - ebs_control_plane.create_volume_reject_disabled
            - ebs_control_plane.cp_metrics

        - order: 3
          at_min: 36
          component: ebs_node_cluster
          event: |
            A rare race condition triggers additional EBS node crashes under extreme replication close-out volume,
            increasing stuck volumes and sharply increasing primary election traffic, causing a second control plane brownout.
          why: |
            High connection churn during the storm raises the probability of the race, producing more node failures and more
            volumes needing re-mirroring. Each blocked volume increases the volume of election negotiations and retries, which
            raises control plane load (partially observable via node_crash_race spikes, increased remirror_activity, and
            renewed thread_pool_saturated logs).
          flows:
            - ec2_volume_io_stuck
            - ebs_elect_primary_ok_load
          rate_multipliers:
            ec2_volume_io_stuck: 1.5
            ebs_elect_primary_ok_load: 1.8
            ebs_node_cluster.node_crash_race: 10.0
            ebs_control_plane.thread_pool_saturated: 1.3
            ebs_api_attach_volume_err: 1.5
          latency_multipliers:
            ebs_elect_primary_ok_load: {p50: 1.4, p95: 1.4}
          one_shots:
            - ref: rds_service.failover_blocked
              count: 2
              hosts: [rds-ctl-01]
          manifestation:
            - ebs_node_cluster.node_crash_race
            - ebs_control_plane.thread_pool_saturated
            - rds_service.failover_blocked
            - ebs_node_cluster.cluster_health

        - order: 4
          at_min: 42
          component: ebs_control_plane
          event: |
            The control plane blocks communication to the degraded cluster to protect the rest of the region, restoring
            regional API health but leaving the affected AZ unable to complete elections and control operations.
          why: |
            Isolating the degraded cluster stops the flood of election and slow upstream work from consuming the shared thread
            pool. This containment is directly observable via partition_status logs and reduced thread_pool_saturated, while
            the AZ continues to see stuck I/O and election failures.
          flows:
            - ebs_elect_primary_err_blocked
            - ebs_api_attach_volume_ok_slow
            - ebs_api_attach_volume_err
          rate_multipliers:
            ebs_elect_primary_ok_load: 0.0
            ebs_elect_primary_err_blocked: 1.0
            ebs_control_plane.thread_pool_saturated: 0.1
            ebs_control_plane.partition_status: 1.0
            ebs_api_attach_volume_err: 0.2
          latency_multipliers:
            ebs_api_attach_volume_ok_slow: {p50: 0.6, p95: 0.6}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ebs_control_plane.cluster_comm_blocked
              count: 1
              hosts: [ebs-cp-02]
          manifestation:
            - ebs_control_plane.cluster_comm_blocked
            - ebs_control_plane.partition_status
            - ebs_control_plane.cp_metrics
            - ebs_control_plane.elect_primary_err

        - order: 5
          at_min: 46
          component: ebs_node_cluster
          event: |
            EBS nodes are reconfigured to back off and avoid futile capacity searches, stabilizing the storm's growth
            but leaving a persistent fraction of stuck volumes awaiting recovery actions not modeled here.
          why: |
            Reducing search frequency lowers internal churn and decreases crash likelihood without immediately freeing capacity,
            so stuck volumes remain but the cluster stops degrading further (observable via reduced remirror_activity and fewer
            node_crash_race logs).
          flows:
            - ec2_volume_io_stuck
          rate_multipliers:
            ebs_node_cluster.remirror_activity: 0.4
            ebs_node_cluster.node_crash_race: 2.0
            ec2_volume_io_stuck: 1.1
          latency_multipliers: {}
          one_shots:
            - ref: ebs_node_cluster.remirror_throttle_enabled
              count: 1
              hosts: [ebs-node-01]
          manifestation:
            - ebs_node_cluster.remirror_throttle_enabled
            - ebs_node_cluster.remirror_activity
            - ebs_node_cluster.cluster_health

      steady:
        - component: ebs_node_cluster
          condition: |
            Re-mirroring storm remains active but stabilized; a persistent subset of volumes remain blocked awaiting writable
            primary election and sufficient free capacity to complete replication.
          user_impact: |
            Affected EC2 instances and RDS databases experience "stuck" I/O and timeouts; some workloads are effectively hung.
          manifestation:
            - ebs_node_cluster.cluster_health
            - ebs_node_cluster.io_blocked_remirror
            - ec2_hypervisor.io_timeout
        - component: ebs_control_plane
          condition: |
            Control plane remains operational for the rest of the region but keeps the degraded AZ partitioned and CreateVolume
            disabled to avoid regional overload.
          user_impact: |
            Customers cannot reliably manage EBS resources in the affected AZ; region-wide APIs mostly work but AZ-specific
            operations and elections fail or are blocked.
          manifestation:
            - ebs_control_plane.partition_status
            - ebs_control_plane.cp_metrics
            - ebs_control_plane.elect_primary_err
            - ebs_control_plane.create_volume_reject_disabled
        - component: rds_service
          condition: |
            A noticeable fraction of single-AZ databases in the affected AZ are stuck on storage I/O; a small number of multi-AZ
            instances require manual intervention due to failover safety checks.
          user_impact: |
            Databases may become inaccessible or error; automatic failover is not always available.
          manifestation:
            - rds_service.rds_health
            - rds_service.txn_error

      feedback_loops:
        - id: remirror_capacity_exhaustion_loop
          desc: |
            Node isolation triggers re-mirroring -> consumes free capacity -> repeated capacity searches -> higher internal load
            and blocked I/O -> more election traffic -> control plane overload -> slower coordination and retries -> sustains storm.
        - id: crash_amplifies_remirror
          desc: |
            High connection churn increases probability of the race-condition crash -> more volumes lose replicas -> more re-mirroring
            demand -> more churn and elections.

      flows:
        - ec2_volume_io_ok
        - ec2_volume_io_stuck
        - ebs_api_create_volume_timeout
        - ebs_api_create_volume_disabled_fast
        - ebs_api_attach_volume_ok_slow
        - ebs_api_attach_volume_err
        - ebs_elect_primary_ok_load
        - ebs_elect_primary_err_blocked
        - rds_txn_stuck
      manifestation:
        - ebs_node_cluster.cluster_health
        - ebs_node_cluster.remirror_activity
        - ebs_control_plane.thread_pool_saturated
        - ebs_control_plane.partition_status
        - ebs_control_plane.create_volume_reject_disabled
        - ec2_hypervisor.io_timeout
        - rds_service.txn_error

  assumptions:
    - >
      Timeline compression: hours of progression are represented as minutes. The two distinct "region-wide API impact"
      periods are modeled as (1) early thread pool saturation driven by long CreateVolume timeouts, and (2) a later
      saturation driven by increased ElectPrimary negotiation load and retries.
    - >
      CreateVolume mitigation fidelity: after CreateVolume is disabled, CreateVolume traffic continues but shifts from
      ebs_api_create_volume_timeout (long latency) to ebs_api_create_volume_disabled_fast (fast reject), making the
      narrative "rejecting new work reduces thread starvation" align with modeled behavior.
    - >
      Piecewise volume sanity (approx):
      Normal (0-20, 20m): ~21.7k logs total (dominated by ec2_volume_io_ok at 300 rpm * 3 logs).
      Failure (20-50, 30m): ~39.1k logs total with event multipliers:
        - 20-28 (8m): storm begins; CreateVolume timeouts active; ~10.0k logs
        - 28-36 (8m): CreateVolume shifts to fast rejects; saturation reduced; ~10.1k logs (+1 one-shot)
        - 36-42 (6m): crash/election surge causes second brownout; ~9.3k logs (+2 RDS one-shots)
        - 42-46 (4m): cluster comms blocked restores region; ~5.1k logs (+1 one-shot)
        - 46-50 (4m): remirror backoff tuned stabilizes storm; ~4.7k logs (+1 one-shot)
      Total scenario estimate: ~60.8k logs (within 20k..100k).
    - >
      Visibility rationale: the incident is diagnosable from frequent metrics (cluster_health/remirror_activity/cp_metrics),
      frequent failure markers (io_blocked_remirror, io_timeout), the CreateVolume fast-reject signature after disable
      (create_volume_reject_disabled), and event-tied operational markers (CreateVolume disabled, cluster comms blocked,
      remirror backoff updated).
