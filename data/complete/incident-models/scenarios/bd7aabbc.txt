scenario:
  id: use1_lambda_frontend_scale_threshold_bug
  title: Lambda cell degradation from frontend scaling threshold causing capacity utilization gap

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    A Lambda cell in us-east-1 normally handles synchronous invocations and asynchronous event deliveries with low error rates.
    When the Lambda Frontend fleet scales beyond a previously untested size for that cell, a latent defect is triggered and the cell
    allocates execution environments but cannot supply enough ready capacity, causing sync invocation retries, errors, and elevated latency.
    Asynchronous sources accumulate backlog and delivery lag; a dependent STS-like SAML federation path later enables throttling as upstream errors persist.

  trigger_desc: |
    The Lambda Frontend fleet in the affected cell scales up to handle a routine traffic increase and crosses a cell capacity threshold
    that triggers a latent software defect, leading to execution environments being allocated but not effectively utilized.

  time:
    total_minutes: 50
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 25}
      f: {start_min: 25, end_min: 50}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        The cell serves steady synchronous invoke traffic and processes asynchronous events with low lag.
        Invocation manager pool stats show adequate ready capacity, frontend metrics show low error rate and stable p95 latency,
        and STS SAML federation requests complete successfully.
      flows: [sync_invoke_ok_n, async_event_delivery_ok_n, sts_saml_federation_ok_n]
      manifestation:
        - lambda_frontend.frontend_metrics
        - invocation_manager.pool_stats
        - lambda_frontend.invoke_success
        - event_router.queue_stats
        - event_router.delivery_success
        - sts_gateway.saml_ok

    f:
      desc: |
        The affected cell enters a degraded state: allocation continues but ready/in-use capacity collapses relative to allocated capacity.
        Synchronous invokes experience retries and higher error rates/latencies, while the event router accumulates backlog and increasing delivery lag.
        Engineers later mitigate by scaling the frontend down below the triggering threshold, improving sync success rates, but async backlog persists
        and drains slowly; downstream STS throttling appears as federation requests continue to encounter upstream Lambda errors.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 25
          component: lambda_frontend
          event: |
            The frontend fleet crosses a new per-cell scaling threshold; shortly afterward sync invocations begin retrying and failing
            with elevated tail latency, and queue backlog indicators for async sources become meaningfully elevated.
          why: |
            Crossing the untested scaling size triggers a latent defect in the cell's capacity/utilization path: environments are allocated,
            but few become ready/in-use, so the frontend cannot obtain sufficient ready environments. This is directly observable via
            increased invoke errors/latency and pool stats showing allocated rising while ready/in-use stays low.
          flows: [sync_invoke_f_err, sync_invoke_f_ok, async_event_delivery_f_fail]
          rate_multipliers:
            async_event_delivery_f_ok: 0.0
            sts_saml_federation_f: 0.0
            invocation_manager.pool_stats: 1.3
            event_router.queue_stats: 1.3
            sts_gateway.throttle_metric: 0.0
          latency_multipliers:
            sync_invoke_f_err: {p50: 2.0, p95: 4.0}
            sync_invoke_f_ok: {p50: 1.6, p95: 2.6}
            async_event_delivery_f_fail: {p50: 1.2, p95: 1.5}
          one_shots:
            - ref: lambda_frontend.scaling_threshold_crossed
              count: 1
              hosts: [lf-2]
            - ref: invocation_manager.capacity_gap_snapshot
              count: 1
              hosts: [im-1]
            - ref: event_router.queue_lag_snapshot
              count: 1
              hosts: [er-1]
          manifestation:
            - lambda_frontend.scaling_threshold_crossed
            - lambda_frontend.invoke_error
            - invocation_manager.provision_blocked
            - invocation_manager.pool_stats
            - event_router.queue_stats

        - order: 2
          at_min: 35
          component: lambda_frontend
          event: |
            Engineers initiate a mitigation by scaling the frontend fleet down below the triggering size; sync invocation error volume decreases,
            and successful sync invokes become more common, though latency remains elevated and async backlog continues to grow.
          why: |
            Reducing the frontend fleet size removes the cell condition that triggers the latent defect, allowing more effective use of existing
            environments and improved readiness. This is directly observable as fewer invoke errors/retries and improving pool readiness.
          flows: [sync_invoke_f_err, sync_invoke_f_ok, async_event_delivery_f_fail]
          rate_multipliers:
            sync_invoke_f_err: 0.5
            sync_invoke_f_ok: 1.3
            async_event_delivery_f_fail: 0.9
            invocation_manager.pool_stats: 1.1
            event_router.queue_stats: 1.2
          latency_multipliers:
            sync_invoke_f_err: {p50: 1.3, p95: 2.0}
            sync_invoke_f_ok: {p50: 1.2, p95: 1.6}
          one_shots:
            - ref: lambda_frontend.scale_down_initiated
              count: 1
              hosts: [lf-1]
          manifestation:
            - lambda_frontend.scale_down_initiated
            - lambda_frontend.invoke_error
            - lambda_frontend.frontend_metrics
            - invocation_manager.pool_stats

        - order: 3
          at_min: 45
          component: event_router
          event: |
            With sync capacity largely improved, the event router begins delivering backlog successfully (still with high lag),
            while some deliveries continue to fail and be retried; STS enables throttling and federation errors become visible.
          why: |
            As the cell becomes more capable of serving new sync invokes, async sources can resume delivery attempts and start draining
            accumulated backlog. Lag remains high because queued events are older. STS throttling is an operational response to persistent
            upstream errors to protect the service; it is observable through STS metrics and failed federation outcomes.
          flows: [async_event_delivery_f_ok, async_event_delivery_f_fail, sts_saml_federation_f, sync_invoke_f_err, sync_invoke_f_ok]
          rate_multipliers:
            sync_invoke_f_err: 0.2
            sync_invoke_f_ok: 1.5
            async_event_delivery_f_fail: 0.6
            async_event_delivery_f_ok: 1.0
            sts_saml_federation_f: 1.0
            sts_gateway.throttle_metric: 1.0
            event_router.queue_stats: 1.2
            invocation_manager.pool_stats: 1.0
          latency_multipliers:
            sync_invoke_f_ok: {p50: 1.0, p95: 1.2}
            async_event_delivery_f_ok: {p50: 1.0, p95: 1.3}
          one_shots:
            - ref: event_router.queue_lag_snapshot
              count: 2
              hosts: [er-1, er-2]
          manifestation:
            - lambda_frontend.async_accepted
            - event_router.delivery_success
            - event_router.queue_stats
            - sts_gateway.throttle_metric
            - sts_gateway.saml_throttled

      steady:
        - component: event_router
          condition: |
            Asynchronous event backlog remains elevated and some deliveries are still retried; successful deliveries carry high lag.
          user_impact: |
            Customers relying on async/streaming event sources see delayed processing and increased end-to-end event latency.
          manifestation:
            - event_router.queue_stats
            - event_router.delivery_retry
            - event_router.delivery_attempt

        - component: lambda_frontend
          condition: |
            Synchronous invocations are mostly successful again but sporadic NoCapacity/timeout errors remain under load.
          user_impact: |
            Some customers still see intermittent invocation failures and elevated tail latency in the affected cell.
          manifestation:
            - lambda_frontend.frontend_metrics
            - lambda_frontend.invoke_error
            - invocation_manager.provision_blocked

        - component: sts_gateway
          condition: |
            STS throttling for SAML federation is active due to upstream Lambda instability.
          user_impact: |
            New SAML federations intermittently fail or are throttled; existing sessions are not modeled here.
          manifestation:
            - sts_gateway.throttle_metric
            - sts_gateway.saml_throttled

      feedback_loops:
        - id: retry_pressure_loop
          desc: |
            Frontend retries increase concurrent demand on scarce ready environments, which can further raise latency and error rates,
            prolonging the time before async backlog can drain.

        - id: backlog_lag_loop
          desc: |
            As async backlog grows, event lag increases and more delivery attempts overlap with ongoing capacity instability, sustaining retries.

      flows:
        - sync_invoke_f_err
        - sync_invoke_f_ok
        - async_event_delivery_f_fail
        - async_event_delivery_f_ok
        - sts_saml_federation_f
      manifestation:
        - lambda_frontend.invoke_error
        - invocation_manager.pool_stats
        - invocation_manager.provision_blocked
        - event_router.queue_stats
        - lambda_frontend.async_accepted
        - sts_gateway.throttle_metric

  assumptions:
    - "Timeline is compressed to 50 minutes while preserving the post-mortem beats (threshold-triggered degradation, mitigation via scale-down, sync recovery preceding async backlog draining)."
    - |
      Failure-phase piecewise breakdown (approx, including retries):
      - Minutes 25-35 (10m): heavy sync failures and growing async backlog; ~2.1k logs/min + ~10 background logs/min; one-shots=3 at minute 25.
      - Minutes 35-45 (10m): mitigation reduces sync failures; ~1.5k logs/min + ~9 background logs/min; one-shots=1 at minute 35.
      - Minutes 45-50 (5m): async drain begins and STS throttling appears; ~1.1k logs/min + ~9 background logs/min; one-shots=2 at minute 45.
    - "Total log estimate is ~65k-75k logs (normal ~26k, failure ~42k, background and one-shots under ~1k), within the 20k-100k target."
    - "Visibility rationale: repeated high-rate signals (lambda_frontend.invoke_error, invocation_manager.provision_blocked, invocation_manager.pool_stats, event_router.queue_stats, and lambda_frontend.async_accepted during draining) make the trigger, mitigation, and remaining backlog diagnosable; mitigation markers and queue snapshots are one-shots to pin phase boundaries."
