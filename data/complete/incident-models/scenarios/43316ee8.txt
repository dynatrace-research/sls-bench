scenario:
  id: gitlab_com_db_data_loss_jan2017_compressed
  title: Write spike, replication stall, and accidental primary data directory deletion

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    GitLab.com serves web/API traffic and stores metadata in a PostgreSQL primary with a streaming replica.
    A sustained write spike (snippet creation/sign-in churn) increases lock contention and slows the primary,
    then replication lag grows until it is effectively stalled. During urgent replica re-seeding and config
    changes, an operator accidentally runs a destructive removal on the primary data directory, taking the site down.
    Service is restored from an older snapshot, leaving an irrecoverable data loss window while replication/backup posture remains broken.

  trigger_desc: |
    Abusive clients begin hammering write endpoints (notably snippet creation and sign-in related writes),
    pushing the primary into lock contention and starting the cascade toward replication lag and emergency operations.

  time:

      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 25}
      f: {start_min: 25, end_min: 50}

        Replication monitoring reports low lag and the replica emits occasional standby heartbeats.
        Backup worker logs may appear but are not actively used by operators during the normal window.
      flows: [read_web_n, write_snippet_n]
      manifestation:
        - pg_primary_db1.sql_query_ok
        - pg_primary_db1.sql_tx_commit
        - rails_web.http_access
        - repl_monitor.repl_lag_ok

    f:
      desc: |

        Replication lag subsequently becomes extreme and on-call attempts to re-seed the replica, encountering WAL sender limits

        forcing a restore from an older snapshot and resulting in a lasting data loss window.

          flows: [abuse_blocked_request_f, write_snippet_ok_f, write_snippet_lock_timeout_f, read_web_f]

          why: |
