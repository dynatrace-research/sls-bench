    As token ownership shifted, some partition keys mapped to new nodes that returned empty results, making specific rows appear missing (not as Cassandra errors).
    Impact included stale/incorrect balances, sporadic login failures, and internal tooling (COps chat/deploys) failing with 404 due to a missing internal network range config key.

  trigger_desc: |
    Operators start a Cassandra scale-up by adding six new nodes, expecting them to remain effectively inactive until data is streamed; auto_bootstrap=false causes them to become active immediately for assigned token ranges.
