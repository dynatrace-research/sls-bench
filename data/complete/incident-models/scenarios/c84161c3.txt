```yaml
scenario:
  id: leap_day_ga_cert_and_bad_ha_plugin
  title: Leap-day GA certificate failure followed by incompatible HA networking plugin blast update

  states: {n: normal, f: failure}

  summary: |
    The cluster normally provisions and heals VMs via FC/HA, with the GA initializing inside each VM and a dependent
    identity service (ACS-like) issuing tokens. At UTC midnight on leap day, GA transfer certificate creation fails,
    causing repeated HA timeouts/reimages and an escalating HI count that forces FC to constrain autonomic operations.
    Operators disable service management to prevent additional deploy/scale churn. A later blast remediation deploys a fixed GA
    but an incompatible HA networking plugin, disconnecting many VMs and producing widespread 503s in dependent services.

  trigger_desc: |
    GA instances attempt to generate a one-year transfer certificate at UTC midnight on leap day and hit an invalid
    "2013-02-29" valid-to date, causing GA initialization to fail immediately.

  time:
    total_minutes: 55
    phases:
      n: {start_min: 0, end_min: 25}
      f: {start_min: 25, end_min: 55}

  phases:
    n:
      desc: |
        Customers perform deploy/scale operations through the Service Management API, which are accepted asynchronously (202)
        and queued as FC VM operations. HA boots VMs and GA initializes successfully and connects to HA. Dependent token issuance
        traffic remains healthy with low error rates.
      flows: [service_mgmt_deploy, vm_bootstrap_success_n, acs_token_success_n]
      manifestation:
        - fabric_controller.fc_cluster_health
        - host_agent.ha_host_status
        - service_mgmt_api.sm_response_success
        - guest_agent.ga_connect_success
        - acs_service.acs_resp_200

    f:
      desc: |
        After UTC midnight on leap day, GA initialization begins failing on transfer certificate creation. HA repeatedly times out
        waiting for GA and reimages VMs, increasing perceived host faults and HI counts until FC constrains autonomic operations.
        Service management is then disabled to reduce additional VM churn. A later blast remediation stops the GA certificate failures,
        but a packaging mistake ships an incompatible HA networking plugin, disconnecting many VMs from the virtual network and causing
        dependent services to return 503/timeouts. A corrected package reduces the network failures but leaves residual HI/corrupted hosts.

      events:
        - order: 1
          at_min: 25
          component: guest_agent
          event: |
            GA initialization starts failing on transfer certificate creation during VM boots, and HA begins timeout/reimage cycles.
          why: |
            The leap-day date arithmetic makes the computed certificate valid-to date invalid; GA exits early and never completes the HA handshake.
            This is directly observable in GA certificate failure logs and subsequent HA GA-connection timeouts.
          flows: [vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail, acs_token_success_f]
          rate_multipliers:
            vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail: 1.2
            vm_bootstrap_fixed_success_f: 0.0
            service_mgmt_request_disabled: 0.0
            acs_token_timeout_f: 0.0
            host_agent.ha_network_plugin_incompatible: 0.0
          latency_multipliers:
            vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail: {p50: 1.0, p95: 1.0}
          one_shots: []
          manifestation:
            - guest_agent.ga_transfer_cert_error
            - host_agent.ha_ga_timeout
            - fabric_controller.fc_cluster_health

        - order: 2
          at_min: 35
          component: fabric_controller
          event: |
            HI threshold is exceeded; FC constrains autonomic operations and operators disable service management to reduce further VM churn.
          why: |
            Repeated clean-boot failures and HA timeouts are misclassified as host faults, raising HI counts until the cluster HI threshold is hit.
            Disabling service management is an operator action to prevent additional deploy/scale operations from triggering more failing VM boots.
          flows: [vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail, service_mgmt_request_disabled]
          rate_multipliers:
            vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail: 0.5
            vm_bootstrap_fixed_success_f: 0.0
            service_mgmt_request_disabled: 1.5
            acs_token_timeout_f: 0.0
            host_agent.ha_network_plugin_incompatible: 0.0
            acs_token_success_f: 0.9
          latency_multipliers:
            vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail: {p50: 1.0, p95: 1.0}
          one_shots:
            - ref: fabric_controller.fc_hi_threshold_hit
              count: 1
              hosts: [fc-01]
            - ref: fabric_controller.fc_autonomic_change_hi
              count: 1
              hosts: [fc-01]
            - ref: service_mgmt_api.sm_disable_action_global
              count: 1
              hosts: [sm-01]
          manifestation:
            - fabric_controller.fc_hi_threshold_hit
            - fabric_controller.fc_autonomic_change_hi
            - service_mgmt_api.sm_response_disabled

        - order: 3
          at_min: 43
          component: host_agent
          event: |
            A blast remediation is deployed to affected hosts: GA certificate failures stop, but an incompatible networking plugin is installed and
            many VMs lose networking, causing dependent token requests to spike in 503/timeouts.
          why: |
            The remediation includes a fixed GA (removing the leap-day cert failure) but mixes an older HA with a newer networking plugin; the plugin
            cannot configure VM virtual networking. The incompatibility is directly observable in HA plugin error logs and the concurrent rise in
            dependent-service 503s.
          flows: [acs_token_timeout_f, acs_token_success_f, vm_bootstrap_fixed_success_f, service_mgmt_request_disabled]
          rate_multipliers:
            vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail: 0.0
            vm_bootstrap_fixed_success_f: 1.0
            service_mgmt_request_disabled: 1.5
            host_agent.ha_network_plugin_incompatible: 30.0
            acs_token_timeout_f: 20.0
            acs_token_success_f: 0.2
          latency_multipliers:
            acs_token_timeout_f: {p50: 1.4, p95: 1.3}
          one_shots:
            - ref: host_agent.ha_blast_update_start_rollback
              count: 7
              hosts: [host-101, host-102, host-103, host-104, host-105, host-106, host-107]
          manifestation:
            - host_agent.ha_blast_update_start_rollback
            - host_agent.ha_network_plugin_incompatible
            - acs_service.acs_resp_503

        - order: 4
          at_min: 50
          component: host_agent
          event: |
            A corrected HA package is blasted out; VM networking begins to recover, reducing but not eliminating dependent-service failures.
          why: |
            Replacing the incompatible plugin/HA combination restores VM network configuration on most hosts.
            Some hosts remain in HI/corrupted intermediate states, so errors persist at a reduced level.
          flows: [acs_token_timeout_f, acs_token_success_f, vm_bootstrap_fixed_success_f, service_mgmt_request_disabled]
          rate_multipliers:
            vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail: 0.0
            vm_bootstrap_fixed_success_f: 1.0
            service_mgmt_request_disabled: 1.2
            host_agent.ha_network_plugin_incompatible: 0.5
            acs_token_timeout_f: 4.0
            acs_token_success_f: 0.6
          latency_multipliers:
            acs_token_timeout_f: {p50: 1.1, p95: 1.1}
          one_shots:
            - ref: host_agent.ha_blast_update_start_corrected
              count: 7
              hosts: [host-101, host-102, host-103, host-104, host-105, host-106, host-107]
            - ref: host_agent.ha_blast_update_complete_corrected
              count: 7
              hosts: [host-101, host-102, host-103, host-104, host-105, host-106, host-107]
            - ref: fabric_controller.fc_manual_repair
              count: 1
              hosts: [fc-02]
          manifestation:
            - host_agent.ha_blast_update_complete_corrected
            - acs_service.acs_resp_503
            - fabric_controller.fc_cluster_health

      steady:
        - component: fabric_controller
          condition: |
            Cluster remains in a constrained state with elevated HI nodes and reduced autonomic servicing/healing.
          user_impact: |
            Capacity and recovery are degraded; some VMs remain unavailable or stuck in intermediate states until manual repair completes.
          manifestation:
            - fabric_controller.fc_cluster_health
            - host_agent.ha_host_status
        - component: service_mgmt_api
          condition: |
            Service management remains disabled or partially effective for the affected cluster, rejecting deploy/scale operations.
          user_impact: |
            Customers cannot reliably deploy, update, or scale; management calls may return 503 even while some workloads continue running.
          manifestation:
            - service_mgmt_api.sm_response_disabled
            - service_mgmt_api.sm_metric
        - component: acs_service
          condition: |
            Dependent service continues to experience intermittent backend unreachability due to remaining disconnected/corrupted VM nodes.
          user_impact: |
            Token requests intermittently fail with 503/timeouts, impacting services that depend on identity and authorization.
          manifestation:
            - acs_service.acs_upstream_unreachable
            - acs_service.acs_resp_503
            - acs_service.acs_metric

      feedback_loops:
        - id: reboot_loop_to_hi_escalation
          desc: |
            GA init failure causes HA to timeout and reimage/retry; repeated clean-boot failures are treated as host faults,
            which increases HI nodes and triggers broader constraints that slow recovery and complicate servicing.
        - id: blast_update_widening_impact
          desc: |
            Attempting to quickly fix skew via blast updates increases simultaneous host transitions; a bad package can abruptly
            expand impact by disconnecting previously healthy VMs.

      flows: [vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail, vm_bootstrap_fixed_success_f, service_mgmt_request_disabled, acs_token_success_f, acs_token_timeout_f]
      manifestation:
        - guest_agent.ga_transfer_cert_error
        - host_agent.ha_ga_timeout
        - fabric_controller.fc_hi_threshold_hit
        - service_mgmt_api.sm_response_disabled
        - host_agent.ha_network_plugin_incompatible
        - acs_service.acs_resp_503

  assumptions:
    - |
      The scenario compresses the real-world multi-hour outage into 55 minutes while preserving ordering and causal structure;
      HA's "25-minute timeout × 3" is represented as shorter but still clearly dominant wait times in vm_bootstrap_leapday_fail.
    - |
      Piecewise volume estimate (rough): normal 0-25m ~800 logs/min (~20k). Failure:
      25-35m ~1450 logs/min (~14.5k) with GA cert failures and HA timeouts;
      35-43m ~1100 logs/min (~8.8k) after HI threshold and mgmt disablement;
      43-50m ~1860 logs/min (~13.0k) during networking plugin incompatibility and ACS 503 spike (GA cert failures suppressed);
      50-55m ~990 logs/min (~5.0k) after corrected blast update.
      Total ~61k logs.
    - |
      One-shot counts: event2 emits 3 markers (FC HI threshold + autonomic constraint + SM disable);
      event3 emits 7 rollback blast-update-start markers; event4 emits 15 markers (7 corrected start + 7 corrected complete + 1 manual-repair).
    - |
      Visibility rationale: early failure is evident via ga_transfer_cert_error + ha_ga_timeout at high rate; management disablement is visible
      via sustained sm_response_disabled; the secondary outage is visible via a burst of ha_network_plugin_incompatible and a concurrent spike in acs_resp_503.
```
